> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #37216](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Sony+Alpha+NEX-6+Control+Wheel+Replacement/37216) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：15 - 20 minutes · 品类：Sony Alpha NEX-6

This guide will illustrate how to dis-assemble the back camera case to gain access the control wheel assembly. Then it will show how to replace the control wheel and adjacent soft keys.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #00 Screwdriver
- iFixit Opening Tool
- Spudger
- Tweezers

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Prior to operating on this device, be sure the camera is turned '''OFF'''. With power, the camera can be a dangerous device to work with.
Turn the camera off by switching the power knob "OFF" text to the white selector dot.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/c51dLFfosLgKDxDH.medium)

### 第 2 步

Flip the camera upside-down and carefully place it on a flat surface.
With the lens facing outward, locate the battery compartment - indicated by a grooved latch.
Push the latch to the right to unlock the battery compartment. The door should flip upward.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pdS6tujYPoRJKDxH.medium)

### 第 3 步

With the compartment open, locate a blue tab shown with spudger
Press the tab to the side (to the left, or toward the camera  wall). This will eject the camera battery.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/hK1xeE3n3NHVOG2M.medium)

### 第 4 步：Battery compartment screws

Remove the two 3.5mm Phillips #00 screws that hold the back case - located inside the battery compartment.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iITdHNwvMVRmsaDr.medium)

### 第 5 步：Bottom screws

Remove the two 3.5mm Phillips #00 screws that hold the back case - located on the bottom of the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wCRexAIFLtWPxeYT.medium)

### 第 6 步：Side screws

Remove the 3.5 mm Phillips #00 screw on the side of camera - located between the charging and HDMI ports.
Remove the 4.0 mm Phillips #00 screw on the side of the camera - located next to the WiFi sign.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qVOGUXERLYUvSEHQ.medium)

### 第 7 步：Dis-attaching procedure

Starting with the battery compartment gently begin to separate the back case front the camera.
Using a plastic opening tool, separate the bottom of the back case from the camera. Then proceed by separating the sides.
Gently lift the back case away from the camera an slide it down to completely remove the back case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IOklr5TSCGXw5kWr.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HU4R2ZvpOrMZwBfG.medium)

### 第 8 步

Pry out the ribbon cable by carefully pushing on its side tabs with a spudger.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sGOOhhWeYfGuIgXn.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cPkEy5RrKO4hVwOV.medium)

### 第 9 步：Control wheel romoval

Using a spudger, lift the metal locking tab located at the top right corner of  control wheel assembly.
Using [product|IF145-020|tweezers], carefully slide the assembly up to release it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Mo5ssXfdPSaHvleV.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JMI6ZRHJYhqEBqQs.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
