> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #117110](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Sony+Alpha+a7s+II+Tripod+Mount+Replacement/117110) · 难度：**简单** · 耗时：4 - 5 minutes · 品类：Sony Alpha a7s II

This guide requires you to take out at least 10 screws. Be extremely careful with where you place them so that you can replace the mount without leaving out the required screws.

## 需要的工具

- J00 JIS Screwdriver
- Tweezers

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Orient your camera so that you are looking at the bottom portion.
Using a JIS #00 screwdriver, remove the 7 screws that are securing the metal plate to the bottom of your camera.
You do not need to remove that recessed screw that is not connected to the bottom metal plate.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/B3hPaRJcDD5WLTVk.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JNXXC6UYcleSHNgV.medium)

### 第 2 步

Remove metal plate. Sometimes this plate can become stuck and hard to remove. Use [product|IF145-020|tweezers] to separate the plate from the bottom of the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kAAHQfVR5VUUyVZb.medium)

### 第 3 步

Flip the plate to the opposite side exposing the aspect that was just inside of the camera.
Use the JIS #00 screwdriver to unscrew the the 3 screws surrounding the tripod mount.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XYMUyB33qbNNP5NE.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the tripod mount from the metal plate.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3SQpT4WGjk5tQOKO.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
