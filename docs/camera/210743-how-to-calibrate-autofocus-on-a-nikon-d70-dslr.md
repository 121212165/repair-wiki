> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #210743](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/How+to+Calibrate+Autofocus+on+a+Nikon+D70+DSLR/210743) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：1 hour · 品类：Nikon D70

If your Nikon D70 is consistently focusing in front of or behind your subject, this guide will show you how to calibrate autofocus on the Nikon D70 DSLR. Over time, the autofocus system can fall out of calibration, causing the camera to focus in front of or behind the intended subject, even when focus confirmation appears correct.

This guide adjusts the internal mirror stoppers to correct autofocus alignment. This method can restore accurate autofocus performance and improve image sharpness, especially when shooting at wide apertures.

This procedure applies to the Nikon D70 and is also similar to autofocus systems used in models such as the Nikon D70s and other early Nikon DSLR cameras. However, this guide has not tested these procedures on other similar Nikon DSLRs and therefore can not guarantee success.

***Caution:*** this repair involves adjusting internal components and using tools near fragile mirror and sensor components. Incorrect adjustments may affect autofocus accuracy or viewfinder alignment.

## 需要的工具

- 2mm Hex Key
- Tripod — Full size
- ISO 12233 Test Chart
- USB-A to mini USB-B cable
- Marker pen

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Print an ***[link|https://www.graphics.cornell.edu/~westin/misc/res-chart.html|ISO 12233|new_window=true]*** focus test chart.
Mount the chart on a flat surface.
Align the camera so the sensor is parallel to the chart, approximately 3–6 feet away.
Let the chart fill the frame.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ioZQUBKvT5Eb4ofk.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/23nJxWCTFNX6ZNlu.medium)

### 第 2 步

Turn the camera on.
Set the lens to its widest aperture by selecting ''M'' or ''A'' on the mode dial.
Select the center autofocus point by using the d-pad.
Take a test photo using autofocus for reference by pressing the shutter.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QSLXgtNfhtfRuQV2.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JTxGjEJVprwODROb.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VGjBAwdAhCRMTdnu.medium)

### 第 3 步

Click the ''Menu'' button and navigate to ''Settings > Mirror lock-up''.
Press the right D-pad button three times until prompted to press the shutter.
Press the shutter button.
***Do not power off the camera***. The mirror may return suddenly and damage tools or internal components.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/LbgBWY51I2bJZSmJ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lAMjokbQkMWGvIAP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bUbHNwQRKRtrjric.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the lens from the camera.
Identify the mirror stoppers on the right side of the mirror box.
Furthest towards the back: ***Autofocus***
Furthest towards the front:   ***Viewfinder***
Record the original stopper positions using a marker pen or reference photo.
Do not touch the mirror or sensor while the mirror is raised.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ShEkjyOqVSlLrWZ2.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xyEFNVPflIFIiRVX.medium)

### 第 5 步

Mark the original position before making any adjustments
Insert a ***[[Item:***2mm Hex Key***|2mm Hex Key|new_window=true]]*** into the autofocus stopper.
Rotate the stopper slightly using very small adjustments.
The stopper is offset from a center pivot, so even a small adjustment will change the autofocus mirror position.
Reattach the lens.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/y4uZNDERMlFxbvWv.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Yl5ELi6ao4tmc6IF.medium)

### 第 6 步

Make sure the lens is attached and all tools are removed ***before turning the camera off.***
Lower the mirror by turning the camera off and back on again.
Take a photo using autofocus.
Record the lens distance markings if available.
Take two additional photos with slight manual focus adjustments, one near and one far.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sJPtQfMXcfwZdaAM.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PAbofT5l6bFOXeWA.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BXPPgiQ3yTXi2JhC.medium)

### 第 7 步

Connect the camera to a computer using a ***[[Item:USB-A to USB-B cable|USB-A to mini USB-B cable|new_window=true]]***.
Change the USB tethering option by navigating to ''Menu > Settings > USB > Mass Storage''.
If available, use an older laptop running ***Nikon Camera Control*** to enable instant USB photo tethering by changing the USB mode to ''PTP''.
Review the images at 100% zoom.
Identify which image is sharpest.
Repeat steps 3-7 until focus improves.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OqjSPZaPtaKJeCYR.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/4XQfKiqxbcAN2gao.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xDUFGfjDZ4QAACv2.medium)

### 第 8 步

Once autofocus calibration is complete, ***only adjust the viewfinder stopper if the viewfinder focus does not match the captured image.***
Repeat steps 3–7 using manual focus only, no autofocus.
When properly calibrated, a green dot will appear in the bottom-left of the viewfinder to confirm focus.
Adjusting the viewfinder stopper may affect autofocus points outside the center area.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FBKnGLWyNGu5CsZs.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2MhKYIU1JJRJtntc.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6MeeHiSgvNobsBcR.medium)

## 完成

Your Nikon D70 autofocus calibration is now complete![br]
[br]
For final confirmation, test the center, left, and right focus points. Use multiple lenses and shooting distances to confirm consistent results.
