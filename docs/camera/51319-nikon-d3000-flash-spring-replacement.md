> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #51319](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Nikon+D3000+Flash+Spring+Replacement/51319) · 难度：**简单** · 耗时：15 minutes · 品类：Nikon D3000

After removing the covering of the flash arm and it's components, remove the metal spring from it's hinge and replace it with a new one.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #00 Screwdriver
- Spudger

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Remove the two 2.5mm x 4mm Philips screws located directly under the flash.
Pop off the covering of the flash to reveal the flash arm spring.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YHjMlyhbWEQJg4sH.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/B322COesFq5pPgGs.medium)

### 第 2 步

Lift the flash arm spring using the plastic spudger and unhinge the spring to remove it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BeHPMrvpZXM5yaty.medium)

### 第 3 步

Remove the spring by sliding it off, then replace it with a new one.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/myGCmcSVwxgdxrHX.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
