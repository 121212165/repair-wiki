> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #32731](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Nikon+D90+Flash+Bulb+Replacement/32731) · 难度：**简单** · 耗时：20 minutes · 品类：Nikon D90

With a few simple tools and parts, this guide will help you replace your broken or burnt out bulb on the Nikon D90 camera. Be careful not to strip the screws as you disassemble the camera. In addition, be careful not to break the bulb or cut yourself on a broken bulb while you are disassembling the camera.

## 需要的工具

- Tweezers
- iFixit Opening Tool
- Phillips #00 Screwdriver

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Open the Flash

Use a plastic opening tool to lift up the flash.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/koZBElMjh2rHOkqK.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZdAMSKAKnOybqISY.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PFxW2wrcWIUn5mvW.medium)

### 第 2 步：Flash Casing Screws

Using a PH00 screwdriver, remove the two 5.3 mm Phillips screws from the flash casing.
Be careful not to strip the screw heads.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YWiYJDOXBvXBEirE.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2qfSQJuWohkwATmm.medium)

### 第 3 步：Removing the Top Flash Casing

Use a plastic opening tool and carefully pry open the top casing of the flash.
Then, lift off the top casing of the flash.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ulBmOxtMRT3GGA62.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qFF5CkVmvtdcquuu.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jjRHcEUByMTK66OK.medium)

### 第 4 步：Removing the Bulb Tape

Carefully unclip the bulb casing from the plastic casing using [product|IF145-020|tweezers].
Remove the black and white tape on the bulb casing.
This tape is what holds the wires and bulb in place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/R551ebkFCBLUvENa.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/k5gpAKSK3WuwXCXQ.medium)

### 第 5 步：Removing the Bulb

Remove the rubber stopper on the left and right side of the bulb.
Separate the rubber stopper from the white and black tape.
The bulb can now be slid out and replaced.
Be careful not to break the bulb or cut yourself on the broken bulb.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/4KhXpwbDTbHmP61I.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tBEFQMkOZOjQEJWM.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/R156hdUxQSSUoRcq.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
