### 第 10 步：Unbuttoning connecters and removal of screws

There are 5 screws left to loosen and 6 connecters to unbutton.
Start removing the 2 connecters in photo 1 with a tweezer, take on the side of the socket without pulling the wires.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cJFlBdBhbyQ2tWeY.medium)

### 第 11 步：Removal of connectors

Remove the two cables from these two connectors, as well as the screw on the right.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Q3SdHfkvPfYIPcaL.medium)

### 第 12 步：Removal of the screws

3 screws to remove, one of which is hidden under the yellow tape.
Remove the plastic cable holder.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/EjG32rxUQP6uyXns.medium)

### 第 13 步：Screw removal

Remove the last screw.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jrHJu2nMv2IwYpnM.medium)

### 第 14 步：Ribbon connecter

Release the ribbon cable by pulling from underneath with a flathead screwdriver or with an unfolded paper clip in the hole provided for this purpose.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SuL5NYsuUbWWFYMc.medium)

### 第 15 步：Clear the card

This is the most delicate part to gently clear the card by moving it without forcing it, until it finds its pivot point.
Be careful, because at the moment there is still a connector connected under the card. However, the cable is long enough to take it out without worry.
At the curvature of the red thread, a small pin visible next to the screw hole is observed. It is probably this that makes it so difficult to remove the card.
There is a second one on the upper part of the card next to the screw that was under the yellow tape.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/beAHUoTlAR1FeX2c.medium)

### 第 16 步：Video connector removal

The connector undoes very easily by levering on each side of the golden connector.
The wide arrow indicates where to pin the flexible cable.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6n63OcuUjAxNGOGb.medium)

### 第 17 步：Card withdrawn

Exchange the deposited card.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kLVoHMjgvKncmhJq.medium)

### 第 18 步：Reassembly of the card

Put the golden connector back on, a slight pressure is enough.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/r2nNtNZ11hgUynlv.medium)

### 第 19 步：Identification of the card centring picot

We see where the plastic pin is located that holds the printed circuit board, next to the screw hole.
Locate the same principle on the top of the card.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IEWY2fYPgQhJyKDZ.medium)

### 第 20 步：Rest from the map

Reinsert the tablecloth into its connector, without forcing, the hole of the tablecloth must be in the plastic pin to be in place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/fwZs3LyKJMIXocbN.medium)

### 第 21 步：Installation of the cables

Be careful, the screw on the side comes to the arrowhead, the other hole must remain free for the screw of the plastic case (I was wrong).

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OktaQvEmZWAUpR13.medium)

### 第 22 步：Reassembly of plastics

Proceeding with the opposite of the video, everything went well.
Just when you put the rear facade back, the screen no longer folded down completely, don't panic, just unlock the three side screws of the card you just changed. The different plastic parts must join without leaving an apparent gap.
Review the end of the video where this problem is well explained, it is thanks to it that I solved this little problem.

### 第 23 步

Use 3M spray mount glue to glue the rubber sheath on the case.
Good luck and good photos.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ckUBsHOl46Pgha53.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow the instructions in reverse order.
