> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #50552](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Canon+EOS+Rebel+T3i+Screen+Replacement/50552) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：20 minutes · 品类：Canon EOS Rebel T3i

Use this guide to replace the LCD screen on your Canon EOS Rebel T3i.

## 需要的工具

- JIS #000 Screwdriver
- Spudger

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Locate the battery compartment on the bottom of the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bAm42cfRNog1r5Tr.medium)

### 第 2 步

Hook your finger onto the compartment door latch.
Open the battery compartment door.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5chA6egdVXQVinPU.medium)

### 第 3 步

Remove the battery from the compartment.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wpYnkD2unGaDqVKU.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the two 3 mm JIS #000 screws. The screws are on opposite sides of the screen.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/S6GdIZPJJMh4l53k.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CxlxWMDCrisxVyNO.medium)

### 第 5 步

Remove the plastic backing.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/LXx6JPZoZUxORlYc.medium)

### 第 6 步

Unplug the blue cable from the back of the LCD.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/KYrTutOvMuwcl3n2.medium)

### 第 7 步

Remove the two 3 mm JIS #000 screws.
Separate the LCD from the plastic housing.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cvERknyo3GPZbbjl.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UxEicPSAOUvtj1og.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
