> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #51816](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Canon+EOS+60D+Motherboard+Replacement/51816) · 难度：**困难** · 耗时：15 - 25 minutes · 品类：Canon EOS 60D

This guide will help you replace the motherboard of your camera.

Be sure to use a magnetic mat or container to hold small screws. Keeping your screws organized will help you return them to their designated holes during reassembly.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #00 Screwdriver
- iFixit Opening Tool
- Precision Tweezers Set

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Take the LCD out of its place to expose the  Phillips  #PH00 screws.
Remove the two 2.5 mm Phillips  #PH00 black screws on both sides of the LCD screen.
Next remove the two  3 mm Phillips  #PH00 black screws near both sides of the base where the screen swivels.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YVohXMlFeyZfoCFw.medium)

### 第 2 步

Using a plastic opening tool, carefully pry off the back cover of the LCD.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/35FvXRWccQVBaMxQ.medium)

### 第 3 步

Remove the 3 mm Phillips #PH00 silver screw on the side of the screen.
Turn the screen to remove the second 3 mm Phillips #PH00 silver screw.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3pnrJ3xDLnqNXvkP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tJKfHT4N2WQVmKxU.medium)

### 第 4 步

Using the [product|IF145-020|tweezers], carefully disconnect the rear connector by pulling it away from the screen.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/NXrBIa1U2LTFkbq6.medium)

### 第 5 步

Now, four 2.2 mm Phillips  #PH00 screws holding a cover around the hinge should be visible.
Turn the swivel around to expose the remaining of the aforementioned screws.
Then use the PH00 screw driver to remove them.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pxRYRxhGCoArKnLC.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Rp2kYhrNYO2OpKEd.medium)

### 第 6 步

The eye piece will slide right off with a firm push upwards.
Remove the 3.4 mm Phillips #PH00 screw using a PH00 screw driver.
Remove the two 9.9 mm Phillips #PH00 screws using the PH00 screwdriver.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qlLZLxOOd4pCOSrL.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/eG3N5cpkW6IWj4Il.medium)

### 第 7 步

Using the PH00 screwdriver, remove the 3.8 mm Phillips #PH00 screw located on the back of the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UGtxhHCxj5gThlx1.medium)

### 第 8 步

Using the plastic opening tool, carefully pull up the rubber grip around the port side of the camera.
Remove the  four different sized screws located beneath the rubber grip.
6 mm Phillips #PH00 screw.
3.4 mm Phillips #PH00 screw.
3.9 mm Phillips #PH00 screw.
2.2 mm Phillips #PH00 screw.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/nSOUG6je3BW13Yjk.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/THBJqtRVSwhZoT1m.medium)

### 第 9 步

Using the plastic opening tool, carefully pry off the the side cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iCpgnWDSYMYRvPIB.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2nDvvZZcXLhllvQQ.medium)

### 第 10 步

Remove two 4.8 mm Phillips #PH00 screws from the memory card side of camera using the PH00 screwdriver.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FXEQiE2pGXYtp11H.medium)

### 第 11 步

Look at the bottom of the camera so the serial number tag reads up right.
Using the PH00 screw driver, remove the top three 3.5 mm Phillips #PH00 screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/laZj3BYRO4WjJLar.medium)

### 第 12 步

Now gently pull the back cover off the camera.
Gently pull the orange wire connected to the motherboard of the camera up and off of the motherboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/oEmNZFoshWbRZvr1.medium)

### 第 13 步

Carefully pry off the LCD screen connector up from the motherboard to disconnect it, using the plastic opening tool.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/X5pmNUIRyvT5XRKF.medium)

### 第 14 步

Remove one 2.8 mm Phillips #PH00 black screw on the side of the swivel assembly by using the PH00 screwdriver.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DuNkTOr3qCR5YYa6.medium)

### 第 15 步

Remove the two 2.8 mm Phillips #PH00 black screws on the back of the swivel assembly using the PH00 screwdriver.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ed2hMf1mFFk3Sawb.medium)

### 第 16 步

Remove the two 4.4 mm Phillips #PH00 screws holding the silver cover using the PH00 screwdriver.
Next lift the cover off of the motherboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FlPZZPnhGXmvy1FX.medium)

### 第 17 步

Use a plastic opening tool to lift the small hinge holding the circled gold ribbon in place, then gently slide the ribbon out of the socket.
Use cation, and pull the ribbon straight out. If damaged the camera may not work the same after reassembly.
Next unscrew the three 4.3 mm Phillips #PH00 screws holding the motherboard down using the PH00 screwdriver.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tHWNjNtBKhY3r2SI.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/u2AGSeH5Hhkp2ZOX.medium)

### 第 18 步

Next the wires are able to come off.
The RED arrows indicate a small hinge locking the wire in. To remove the wire, follow the same steps taken to remove the wire in the prior steps.
The ORANGE markers indicate that you need to lift the wire up and off of the motherboard. Using the opening tool, follow the same steps used to remove the swivel screen wire.
Next it is recommended that you use [product|IF145-020|tweezers] to carefully remove all hinged wires as demonstrated by the YELLOW arrow on the second image in this step.
It is crucial that you move slowly and carefully! If one of the wires gets damaged, the camera might not work properly after reassembly.
You must carefully remove these wires by pulling them straight out of the socket they're seated in.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/rSJGg3hHCTYBkass.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UMVYbxY4ZNqxaPed.medium)

### 第 19 步

With the motherboard lifted, remove the indicated wire by using a plastic opening tool.
With the first bottom wire removed, the motherboard can be lifted higher, showing two more hidden wires.
Remove the wire indicated by the ORANGE arrow by undoing a hinge and sliding the wire out.
Remove the wire indicated by the YELLOW arrow by gently pulling it straight out of the socket it is seated in.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/EXMHEquYsTOQVrBU.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/MKFpRbFxIjliYj2o.medium)

### 第 20 步

With every wire properly removed, the motherboard can now be lifted out of the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/NWXIy3iMqy6jqSbI.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
