> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #25685](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Nikon+D5100+LCD+Replacement/25685) · 难度：**Very difficult** · 耗时：15 - 20 minutes · 品类：Nikon D5100

The LCD unit is the main component of the digital display on the D5100. This guide will teach you how to remove and replace it.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #000 Screwdriver
- Spudger
- Soldering Iron

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Use a Phillips #000 screwdriver to remove the four screws around the perimeter of the LCD panel.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JHBWxvnwPtDQrKsO.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TaRGFBihUAAbyxnv.medium)

### 第 2 步

Using a fingernail or the flat edge of a spudger inserted into the seam, separate the LCD bevel from the main panel body.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xOZD2QxDcJXSICEH.medium)

### 第 3 步

Use a spudger to remove the ribbon cables on the LCD's circuit board by flipping up their plastic lock-bars and removing gently pulling them out of their ports.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dGxlOpKt4RkjouZU.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5ATxgpNxlgGn2NeA.medium)

### 第 4 步

Use a spudger to disconnect the 16-pin cable by gently pulling it upward, away from the circuit board, until it is removed from its port.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RosNOU3TwWBCKNFE.medium)

### 第 5 步

[link|https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/How+To+Solder+and+Desolder+Connections/750|new_window=true|Desolder] the 3 wires (red, yellow, and black) connecting the LCD board to the main camera body.
Soldering/desoldering presents potential for device damage and burns. Take all necessary precautions, and always solder/desolder in a well-ventilated workspace.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UpiuNLq1rLWOxPUE.medium)

### 第 6 步

The LCD assembly can now be fully separated from the main camera body.
Remove the plastic bezel and metal bracket from the LCD itself to complete the removal process.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DpUsS3fPrpfpDMqN.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xWDxsMYl4ppfmZXt.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
