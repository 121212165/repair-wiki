## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Turn on the camera, go to the menu, and then to Setup Menu 4 (wrench icon with 4 dots). From there, scroll down (or use the touchscreen) to the fifth item: "Clear all camera settings". Confirm this with OK. Wait a moment, and you are done.
Note: This guide applies to the 70D version 1.1.3

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3UQLd5yYcr6CaD4p.medium)

## 完成

Follow the steps in reverse order to reassemble your device.
