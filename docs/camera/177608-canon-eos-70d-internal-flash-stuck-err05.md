title: "Canon EOS 70D - Internal flash stuck (Err05)"
---

> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #177608](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Canon+EOS+70D+-+Internal+flash+stuck+(Err05)/177608) · 难度：**简单** · 耗时：10 minutes · 品类：Canon EOS 70D

If the built-in flash will not open, in most cases it is due to dirt or debris.

To the video:

[video|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bLfktAujDrY]

## 需要的工具

- Schraubenzieher Kreuzschlitz
- Schraubenzieher, Schlitz, zum Hebeln
- Tweezers

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Manually opening the flash

Turn the camera on and insert your fingernail or a flathead screwdriver between the camera and the internal flash. Now, using your other hand, press the "flash open" button on the front of the camera. This should allow you to open the flash manually.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/s2pPrOo5Q4LBHK2X.medium)

### 第 2 步：Release the screws

Remove the two small screws directly under the flash. Then, carefully pull off the cover. You may need to release it from the small square openings at the bottom.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CAb2BVIwkPTqwBoK.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/P6ZYeIZtw1YmOTQR.medium)

### 第 3 步：Loosen the screw

Now look at the flash from the side. On only one side there is a silver screw with a spring around it. Turn this screw slightly out and then back in, close the flash, and see if it can already be opened again. The background is that there are often impurities on this screw.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/aIolQe5gEFuqEJpi.medium)

### 第 4 步：Remove the screw and clean the screw

If the flash opens: Congratulations. Screw it back in and you are finished. If it still does not work, then release the spring and subsequently remove the screw. Clean it and insert it again. Tension the spring again and test it once more.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xilGcAe6a2X3iVCs.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dnkuHdRdLMeyngKg.medium)

### 第 5 步：Reinstalling the cover

If it does not work, likely only Canon Support can help at this point. Important: Do not tighten the spring too much. If it works: Press the cover back onto the flash until it clicks into place. Then test one or two more times to see if it still opens. Now screw the two screws back in. DONE

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/B2i2QifQopruVaHv.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/34ohupBSKLlp4uUM.medium)

### 第 6 步：DONE
