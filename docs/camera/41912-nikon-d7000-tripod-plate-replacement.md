> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #41912](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Nikon+D7000+Tripod+Plate+Replacement/41912) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：30 - 35 minutes · 品类：Nikon D7000

Use this guide to replace the tripod plate.

''Note:'' You'll need JIS screwdrivers for this repair. Regular Phillips screwdrivers have a cross pattern with rounded inner edges and won't fully fit the slots in the JIS screws. JIS screwdrivers instead have a straight cross pattern which makes much better contact with the screw head and is made for the high torque you will need to loosen the screws.

## 需要的工具

- JIS Driver Set
- Metal Spudger
- Phillips #0 Screwdriver
- Spudger
- Tweezers
- Phillips #000 Screwdriver

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

When loosening the screws, start pressing the screwdriver firmly for the first 1-2 turns (highest torque) until the screw is 'unlocked'. Failing to do so can damage the screw head and make it impossible to disengage that screw.
Remove the following 5 screws securing the bottom cover to the camera body:
Four 6 mm J000 screws.
One 8 mm J000 screw (under lens mount).

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cm2ORulUQgTIcLmD.medium)

### 第 2 步

Remove three 6 mm J000 screws from under the battery cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QrvtqxRGqmKLmUBP.medium)

### 第 3 步

Gently pull the bottom cover off of the camera body.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1GCOjoFZbEViDOW4.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PJGgX3SaLO4TUbMl.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the following screws from the port area:
Two 3.5 mm J000 screws.
One 6 mm J000 screw (right above ports).

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XVgnRPoHTfHutPvM.medium)

### 第 5 步

Peel the rear rubber piece off with [product|IF145-020|tweezers].

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HkWQ6gc1GHGvddu4.medium)

### 第 6 步

Remove one 4.25 mm J000 screw from behind the rear rubber piece.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6XxLbBTXnoWYi1Ge.medium)

### 第 7 步

Pull the eyepiece cover up to remove it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TEv5KiI1DkUae1PV.medium)

### 第 8 步

Remove two 6 mm J000 screws from behind the eyepiece cover.
On D7100 models, there is an additional screw holding the back plate on. This screw is located in the diopter (dial with + - on it). This screw can be accessed by peeling up this sticker on the diopter.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kmD2nMN41RJFCpFK.medium)

### 第 9 步

Open the SD card cover by pushing slightly down with your thumb and sliding up.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xDPUe1oxYB1bm3KO.medium)

### 第 10 步

Remove four 6 mm J000 screws that secure the SD card cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vLRVX4NIPSFASgd1.medium)

### 第 11 步

Gently pull the SD card cover off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/nMT4dsBPdeK6J21C.medium)

### 第 12 步

Remove the following screws that secure the back case:
One 6 mm J000 screw.
One 3 mm J000 screw.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/EWyUIHjVmBVOgqYS.medium)

### 第 13 步

Carefully lift the back case off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pNJYwvZBCDlCCpZi.medium)

### 第 14 步

Disconnect the following two ribbon cables from the motherboard.
See our [guide|25629|Cable Connector Guide|64969] for help with ribbon cable connectors!

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lh3Oft4G2DQp6i2E.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FXEpWEbpTIw1UJYn.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/aTqIKEtXXMoEpEf4.medium)

### 第 15 步

Remove the following screws securing the left motherboard shield:
One 2 mm J000 screw (on the bottom).
One 3.5 mm J000 screw (on the back).
Be careful of the grounding clips on the motherboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/hiVpQMmmHvQwywQT.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jaHEmXKNNr2MMIgV.medium)

### 第 16 步

Gently pry and lift the left motherboard shield off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/fK2lsUZIQM6RHB4T.medium)

### 第 17 步

Remove one 3.5 mm J000 screw securing the right motherboard shield.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/nTEWTNAKao3GrCri.medium)

### 第 18 步

Gently lift the right motherboard shield off with the tip of a spudger.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/NNACDft5njSv6EL4.medium)

### 第 19 步

Disconnect remaining ribbon cables.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wgv453L4WEkbAppj.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HBMtgXGXO32h4rxn.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/uYtnEvYSojhopC2Q.medium)

### 第 20 步

Disconnect remaining ribbon cables.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vrKgmlOCkQZsnoSR.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tLGYNWDruWlMGiho.medium)

### 第 21 步

Carefully disconnect the red connector with tweezers.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bPYkPsiLyFvMgpFg.medium)

### 第 22 步

Remove four 3.5 mm J000 screws securing the motherboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/4Iurv4DleT5RAXaJ.medium)

### 第 23 步

Gently pull the port side cover away from the motherboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FbPCIAVSAoAmHKEy.medium)

### 第 24 步

Lift the motherboard off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5aopPFIduUGoVfRG.medium)

### 第 25 步

Remove four 6 mm J000 screws securing the tripod plate.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/D6B3TYP6DEBnulQZ.medium)

### 第 26 步

Carefully lift the tripod plate from the backside of the camera and slide out the grounding clip from under the lens mount.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZQP4OJa5LfGHp4pC.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
