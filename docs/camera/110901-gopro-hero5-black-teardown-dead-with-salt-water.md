> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #110901](https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/GoPro+Hero5+Black+Teardown+-+dead+with+salt+water/110901) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：No estimate · 品类：GoPro Hero5 Black

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

A faulty cover allowed seawater to enter - it destroyed the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JNWxEkRg6Cx5j1Dc.medium)

### 第 2 步

Front cover removal. It is secured with a very strong adhesive.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/KoQaDqLrCfFtKAS3.medium)

### 第 3 步

Seawater has corroded everything. The circuit is completely damaged.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OlDpOOOsKEBGvVke.medium)

### 第 4 步

Battery and rear LCD compartment fully contaminated. Battery has discharged.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Gw4XNmMFqStY2gUF.medium)

### 第 5 步

The CCD part was not opened. I do not know if water got in. Circuits destroyed.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZBTgjbPIjup23RtE.medium)

### 第 6 步

Corroded and damaged USB and HDMI connectors.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gXwNVqZK5KkWfrRy.medium)

### 第 7 步

Everything contaminated. Everything destroyed!

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FaUjiERUsSFkhuxx.medium)

### 第 8 步

Main board disassembled - some components removed.
Qualcomm QCA9377 - Wi-Fi/Bluetooth SoC

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VPlhIYXJhxxeP6eO.medium)

### 第 9 步

Mainboard disassembled - processor.
Ambarella A9SE Sports and Flying Camera Processor
Conexant CX20811 - 4 Channel High Performance Microphone Array ADC with Programmable Pre-amplifier and Class D PWM Out

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SGTNHYDbabkcVKXO.medium)

### 第 10 步

Disassembled 4K camera.
