title: "Nikon D90 Rear Case Replacement"

> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #21495](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Nikon+D90+Rear+Case+Replacement/21495) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：No estimate · 品类：Nikon D90

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #000 Screwdriver

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Slide the eyepiece up and off the viewfinder.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OLtVPyAUOLcQIfFy.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BhWW4DcOBnURuA22.medium)

### 第 2 步

Using a PH000 screwdriver, remove the two 4.8mm Phillips screws from the back of the viewfinder.
Some screws may be JIS. Be wary of this if your PH000 does not feel like it is properly gripping the screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Iivs3FIQFsTNmOEl.medium)

### 第 3 步

Remove the two 6.4mm Phillips screws on the right side of the camera, near the SD card slot.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/x3tSAmwZsPZTdecu.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the two 6.4mm Phillips screws on the left side of the camera, near the I/O ports.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/unoku3wppXQUUwER.medium)

### 第 5 步

On the bottom of the camera,
remove the two 5.3mm Phillips screws
and the other two 6.3mm Phillips screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/EOxKthUDiC3UEkGR.medium)

### 第 6 步

Using a spudger, separate the bottom panel from the rear case just enough to clear the two clips on the rear casing in the next step.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PXwn4qHfW62XEaNL.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VM5gvfh23tpTklos.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IQLm5GpUvMYJnmfX.medium)

### 第 7 步

Lift the rear case off of the back of the camera.
Don't pull the casing completely off yet, as the ribbon cables are still connected.
Be mindful of the two clips, making sure to keep the bottom panel far enough away to clear them.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3JfhoJpJnQVSadDW.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OdGIVINJDqi6OYMB.medium)

### 第 8 步

Set the rear casing off to the side so you can access the ZIF connectors.
The orientation shown in the picture seems to be the best position for the casing to sit comfortably without stressing the ribbon cable connections.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wIUYqRdIvflqHIAw.medium)

### 第 9 步

Use a spudger to lift the black tab on the [link|https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Recognizing+&+Disconnecting+Cable+Connectors/25629#s64969%7CZIF connector|new_window=true|new_window=true|ZIF connector].
Disconnect the ribbon cables from their respective ZIF connectors on the logic board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sWmpirPRRwLcgUQr.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bJdlfcXTLSiDgXZP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/4NcPqtQdidSN56hd.medium)

### 第 10 步

Separate the rear casing from the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2ZYgSLwhPdlOIZ1T.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
