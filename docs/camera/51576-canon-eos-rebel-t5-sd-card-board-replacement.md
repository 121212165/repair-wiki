> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #51576](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Canon+EOS+Rebel+T5+SD+Card+Board+Replacement/51576) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：45 minutes - 1 hour · 品类：Canon EOS Rebel T5

If your camera is no longer reading SD cards, you may find that you need to replace your card reader. This guide will show you the steps necessary.

## 需要的工具

- JIS #000 Screwdriver
- Fingers
- Spudger
- iFixit Opening Tool
- Precision Tweezers Set

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Lay the camera on its back so that the card/battery door is facing you.
Using your fingernail, slide the tab on the battery door up to release the battery door latch.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JZpuhYn5aNx2a5k5.medium)

### 第 2 步

Lift the now free card/battery door to reveal the battery compartment.
Push the gray lever to the right side so that the battery can be removed and replaced with a new one.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ImYNPluw4SOitEDE.medium)

### 第 3 步

Lay the camera on its back so that the card/battery door is facing you.
Using your fingernail, slide the tab on the battery door up to release the battery door latch.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ELj2wCKA6UFw2nXe.medium)

### 第 4 步

Open the battery compartment by lifting the card/battery door. You will see a thin SD Card slot below the battery.
Push in the old SD card until it clicks to remove it and then push in the new card until it clicks into place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tZ2XMUQTSc6NKtcR.medium)

### 第 5 步

Turn the camera so that the back is facing to your right.
Remove the 5mm JIS #000 screw above the rubber USB port cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pL6vROgghEGJB2p2.medium)

### 第 6 步

Remove the rubber piece.
Unscrew the two screws under the rubber cover to completely remove the rubber cover piece.
It is held in place by a notched tether, but can be easily manipulated to slide out.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QCLgfQBmWFJQQwmh.medium)

### 第 7 步

Turn your camera so that the bottom is facing you.
Remove the four 5mm JIS #000 screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JGvt2omhPKEK5RyX.medium)

### 第 8 步

Now turn the camera so that the front is facing towards your right.
Remove the two remaining 5mm JIS #000 screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ISEoqXlHElNZTyxX.medium)

### 第 9 步

Now that all of the exterior screws have been removed, grip the camera firmly on both sides.
Gently pry the back panel from the main body of the camera.
There are two ribbon cables that need to be removed in order for the back panel to come free from the camera body, as indicated.
This back panel contains various control components of the camera as well as the LCD screen.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/MsAeFqtNGjb34bSj.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/f64UqWVwpAtPgpEo.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/A33ZdIWoKkStvY3V.medium)

### 第 10 步

Remove the four silver 4mm JIS #000 screws connecting the LCD screen to the back of the camera.
Be sure to lift the ribbon cable to remove the remaining hidden black 4mm JIS #000 screw.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/A3slMXZGSjJNvfgS.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PxEt3OLOxKq6Gupv.medium)

### 第 11 步

The LCD screen can now be lifted from the right side to be replaced.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/WLsco3XbxpRXRBMU.medium)

### 第 12 步

Remove all ribbon cables with the plastic spudger.
Gently pry up the black plastic tabs to slide out the ribbon cables
Take note of this black cable and where it connects to the motherboard. This is a fiber optic cable which is used for the camera flash. It will need to be reconnected after replacing the motherboard.
When reconnecting the fiber optic cable, simply push the black cable into the hole in the connector and push it all the way in.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/E3xJNDcNIWcW65NV.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tPwfHFwe2iETuvNA.medium)

### 第 13 步

Turn your camera so that the top is facing you.
Remove the two black 5 mm JIS #000 screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bwY4L1ruPBK2m1jF.medium)

### 第 14 步

Turn the grip side toward you.
Remove the rubber grip, using the plastic spudger.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iR5eTXmhxmFrsCxF.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HsM1vLY1VtQOefip.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RPe5ZDjWMFc1qgpo.medium)

### 第 15 步

Remove the black 5 mm JIS #000 screw, located under the grip.
Remove the three 3.5mm JIS #000 screws on the bottom of the case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YjAfcetVU655pPMe.medium)

### 第 16 步

Remove the top panel of the device.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jlDlMIYOoKcKYTnC.medium)

### 第 17 步

Remove the front panel of the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ugfNSgnwDxFjmyi2.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gNem1Hd3xWCXO2xM.medium)

### 第 18 步

Remove the five silver 5mm JIS #000 screws.
The motherboard can now be removed.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/boa6RZLDuDIAb2FR.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cN3PYQ6qSyovMSAv.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OFWsUITrSB6laMmi.medium)

### 第 19 步

Using a pair of tweezers, remove the tape covering the SD card board ribbon cable.
Using the plastic spuger, lift up the black locking tab on the SD card board ribbon cable and disconnect the ribbon cable.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CuMAObbX2xO2LcIB.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PCXGtIBhqffRcXBX.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VPc4axpJrUdpDIRy.medium)

### 第 20 步

Remove the three 3mm JIS #000 screws connecting the SD card reader to the back of the device.
Carefully lift the SD card board out of the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/fdAeLARevBWSeGmc.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/KZRHixY6VaZCqOTQ.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
