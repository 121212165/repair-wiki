> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #50558](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Canon+EOS+Rebel+T3i+Top+Cover+Replacement/50558) · 难度：**困难** · 耗时：10 minutes · 品类：Canon EOS Rebel T3i

Use this guide to replace the top cover on your Canon EOS Rebel T3i.

NOTE: On some variations of the Canon EOS Rebel T3i, the cables that connect the top cover to the DC PCB board are soldered on versus connected to it with a connector. If you are just removing the top cover to make other repairs, you can just keep those cables attached and carefully remove the top cover to get it out of the way. If you are replacing the top cover, you'd have to solder the cables (if they are soldered on).

## 需要的工具

- JIS #000 Screwdriver
- Spudger
- Tweezers

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Locate the battery compartment on the bottom of the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bAm42cfRNog1r5Tr.medium)

### 第 2 步

Hook your finger onto the compartment door latch.
Open the battery compartment door.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5chA6egdVXQVinPU.medium)

### 第 3 步

Remove the battery from the compartment.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wpYnkD2unGaDqVKU.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the two 3 mm JIS #000 screws.
Some variations of this camera may have one 3mm and one 6mm screws.
If the rubber eyepiece mount is still on the camera, remove it before attempting to separate the case halves.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/4sTqkPidvIg2GBnL.medium)

### 第 5 步

Remove the three 3 mm JIS #000 screws.
Remove the single 6 mm JIS #000 screw.
Some variation of this camera have two 3mm and two 6mm screws

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/weM3kDdvsXRlECB1.medium)

### 第 6 步

Peel back the grip tape with your fingernail.
Depending on the age of the camera, the grip tape will stick back in place with no need for extra glue.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TFNZcYlMIZT41QXm.medium)

### 第 7 步

Remove the four 5 mm JIS #000 screws.
Some variation of this model may have three 5mm screws and one 6mm

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5PXd6okOqdnWOQVI.medium)

### 第 8 步

Slide off the right side of the camera.
Do not completely separate the body yet. There are still wires that must be disconnected. If the body is pulled apart too quickly, it may cause damage.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/KgPsDxMQDIid1Fyy.medium)

### 第 9 步

Lift off the USB cover.
Do not completely remove the back of the camera. There are still wires that must be disconnected. Removing the back too quickly may cause damage to wires.
When lifting the cover, the only thing resisting your tug is the plug cover stopper. Just pull, you'll be fine.
When replacing it, having the covers snug on the plate will make it easier to push the whole cover on

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6uu2Ds4yxjFJbB5C.medium)

### 第 10 步

After separating the back, gently lift off one side of the light blue wire with the spudger until the connector is totally removed.
You may feel a slight resistance as you pull the top of the back cover off, it will click but that is fine.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Tk1ukf1qVn4WgFyu.medium)

### 第 11 步

With the spudger, remove the orange ribbon cable leading to the circuit board from the back of the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tmuFGpLsJyRYcvA4.medium)

### 第 12 步

Remove the two 6 mm JIS #000 screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qJBZchjwwAK2E2Qc.medium)

### 第 13 步

Remove the single 6 mm and two 3 mm JIS #000 screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QCKxhgqCVyY3RwYQ.medium)

### 第 14 步

Use the spudger to lift the plastic under the tripod mount.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CXVeTXShYsXxQXth.medium)

### 第 15 步

Lift the front cover off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/W1egCREKWYuNbCil.medium)

### 第 16 步

Remove the single 4 mm JIS #000 screw located near the strap mount.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/h4P3HTvCVPX3hKGZ.medium)

### 第 17 步

On the opposite end of the camera, unscrew the screw next to the power switch using the same tool.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FPhqmaY3aXtSEwWF.medium)

### 第 18 步

Remove the two 5 mm JIS #000 screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/fLNV6QKlHFNLIexX.medium)

### 第 19 步

Disconnect this flat cable with a spudger

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/poBNDXjvfdHjjfKi.medium)

### 第 20 步

Disconnect this tiny plug. Make sure to only pull on the white plastic.  You can use a dull single-sided razor blade to pry the white plastic connector from its base.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/eYasckPiGmVZiXuM.medium)

### 第 21 步

Remove the single 11 mm JIS #000 screw securing the dioptric adjustment knob.
When replacing, make sure you do not tighten the screw too much, as the knob will be locked in position if you do.
Before replacing the knob/screw, make sure the grey plastic mount (screw hole area) is aligned with the outer case. If it is not, it means the  lens and the knob are not lined up. With a flat screw driver, give the grey plastic a few rotations, and it should fall in place, and realign properly.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GZIaqEoDRvhrGJ43.medium)

### 第 22 步

Use the [product|IF145-020|tweezers] to disengage the yellow and red wires from the camera body.
Some variations of this camera may not have unplugable wires. Make sure to take note of which cable goes where if you decide to remove the solder.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1tMIfvSucjVCIw3Q.medium)

### 第 23 步

Lift the top casing up and away from the camera.
Make sure the black plastic cable goes into the white plastic connector, as shown on the picture. This is a fiber optic cable that the camera flash needs in order to function. The cable can simply be pushed into the connector.
Here is a view of the black plastic bit. It is on the underside of the top cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/usiSPuTJqFAwYmWD.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SYtTLEuDI11VN2E4.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IgDptTTdfoSarpXM.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
