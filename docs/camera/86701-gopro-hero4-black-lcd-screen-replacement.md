> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #86701](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/GoPro+Hero4+Black+LCD+Screen+Replacement/86701) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：15 - 30 minutes · 品类：GoPro Hero4 Black

## 需要的工具

- Metal Spudger
- Phillips #00 Screwdriver
- iFixit Opening Tool
- Tweezers
- iOpener

## 需要的配件

- GoPro Hero4 Black LCD & Midframe Assembly
- GoPro Hero4 LCD Assembly

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Remove the faceplate by carefully prying around the edge using a plastic opening tool. Loosen the clips on the edge, then pull the faceplate off.
Use the [product|IF145-020|tweezers] to lift the faceplate  from the rest of the device.
The power button on the front of the GoPro may pop off. Ensure to align it properly during reassembly.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Ad6RKFvVK1EuCJCA.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/AwnGRbTI1iXlvZOw.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2IgKYe2mHSVjYEtP.medium)

### 第 2 步

Using a #00 Phillips head screwdriver, remove the following screws:
One 4.6 mm screw
Three 8.2 mm screws
Two 6.1 mm screws

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3dRO6AHP5BsDgpCX.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1Jt5QZUvVuxhAvbh.medium)

### 第 3 步

Insert a metal spudger between the back plastic housing and the camera board assembly. Working around the edge to be careful of the inner components, carefully remove the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/waYvGOsr3maDBtKY.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tNkFMdWsAoJmfsLl.medium)

### 第 4 步

Using tweezers, remove the ribbon strip by pulling on it. This will remove the connection.
Push down on the three wires with the metal spudger and the clip will disconnect from the motherboard.
The clip must be pulled '''up and away''' from the motherboard. Attempting to slide it out will damage the clip.
During reassembly, remove the black tape on the ribbon cable, turn the camera upside down so you can see the port, and reinsert the cable with tweezers. It's a lot easier!

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FqANrFFnGecKWn5M.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bAVcUhNIFMsKANof.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XnLdBPQidvnpP5Sg.medium)

### 第 5 步

Open the clip that is located on the motherboard
Use a pair of [product|IF145-020|tweezers] to pull the ribbon from the clip

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GbRI1ut4uCIva6fI.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qX5ouf3hMPAISr2Q.medium)

### 第 6 步

Before you start this step flip the section holding the LCD screen over
Place the iOpener on the LCD screen cover to release the glue
Lift the covering of the LCD screen cover off with a pair of tweezers

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UGHCiTkliWesSamV.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ilmt6TAV4HPXYG4S.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/EHKCUTcIM4RoO4JW.medium)

### 第 7 步

Use a pair of tweezers to lift the ribbon through the opening and lift all the way up to the LCD screen
A section of our LCD screen ribbon was broken so it is not visible from the image.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/eXBQqYtLYjPdmcOW.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/rVWnxQRnVDCfmsSD.medium)

### 第 8 步

Place the iOpener on the LCD screen to remove the glue
Use a pair of tweezers to left the LCD screen off the device

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/uZKna35OYgZWZcur.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/MLcNmYwAdnkNTP5m.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
