> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #117106](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Sony+Alpha+a7s+II+Diopter+Replacement/117106) · 难度：**简单** · 耗时：5 minutes · 品类：Sony Alpha a7s II

The diopter is unable to be calibrated, creating an optimal focus issue for the users to match their eyesight.

## 需要的工具

- JIS 00 Screwdriver Bit
- Tweezers

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Orient your camera so that you are looking at the posterior portion.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/h3NOW2tWEYLl5p3X.medium)

### 第 2 步

Place each of your thumbs on the interior portions of the eyepiece cup, pressing down and pushing up.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/uTCrjkuvXMmKqBtv.medium)

### 第 3 步

Slide the eyepiece off by carefully pulling it upward.
Be careful not to come into contact with the viewfinder lens as the lens can be easily scratched.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1UDIyiZyZkhDUEW6.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the screw from the middle of the diopter with the JIS #00 screwdriver.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JCmuJvtCPImIrD4F.medium)

### 第 5 步

Remove the diopter with your hands. If the diopter is at all stuck you can use the [product|IF145-020-2|pointed tweezers] to help aid in the removal process.
Be careful not to let fingers or tweezers come into contact with the viewfinder lens as the lens can be easily scratched.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pQErENJss4uwHA1Q.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
