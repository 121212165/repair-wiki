> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #86215](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/GoPro+Hero4+Black+Battery+Housing+and+Rear+Frame+Replacement/86215) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：15 - 25 minutes · 品类：GoPro Hero4 Black

Replace the battery housing when the device will not power on but the battery has been verified to work.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #00 Screwdriver
- iFixit Opening Tool
- Metal Spudger
- Tweezers

## 需要的配件

- GoPro Hero4 Black Battery Enclosure
- GoPro Hero4 Battery
- GoPro Hero4 Black Shutter Button Board

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Remove the faceplate by carefully prying around the edge using a plastic opening tool. Loosen the clips on the edge, then pull the faceplate off.
Use the [product|IF145-020|tweezers] to lift the faceplate  from the rest of the device.
The power button on the front of the GoPro may pop off. Ensure to align it properly during reassembly.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Ad6RKFvVK1EuCJCA.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/AwnGRbTI1iXlvZOw.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2IgKYe2mHSVjYEtP.medium)

### 第 2 步

Using a #00 Phillips head screwdriver, remove the following screws:
One 4.6 mm screw
Three 8.2 mm screws
Two 6.1 mm screws

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3dRO6AHP5BsDgpCX.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1Jt5QZUvVuxhAvbh.medium)

### 第 3 步

Insert a metal spudger between the back plastic housing and the camera board assembly. Working around the edge to be careful of the inner components, carefully remove the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/waYvGOsr3maDBtKY.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tNkFMdWsAoJmfsLl.medium)

### 第 4 步

Using tweezers, remove the ribbon strip by pulling on it. This will remove the connection.
Push down on the three wires with the metal spudger and the clip will disconnect from the motherboard.
The clip must be pulled '''up and away''' from the motherboard. Attempting to slide it out will damage the clip.
During reassembly, remove the black tape on the ribbon cable, turn the camera upside down so you can see the port, and reinsert the cable with tweezers. It's a lot easier!

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FqANrFFnGecKWn5M.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bAVcUhNIFMsKANof.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XnLdBPQidvnpP5Sg.medium)

### 第 5 步

Using a #00 phillips head screwdriver, remove the two 3.0mm screws
The top screw is missing from our device, but it is in the approximate location of the top circle.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bxQgxKZhteBekLc4.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/x5ZS6taU6kKiHqRZ.medium)

### 第 6 步

Be sure to lift the battery cover before proceeding.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/uaBJiVjMPeJE31iM.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tFO5ZiDHYIRPXsQl.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lIBw2D6BRKX2WyAM.medium)

### 第 7 步

Using plastic opening tool, pry battery housing from the rear frame.
When prying the housing off, the record and setting buttons may fall off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZJSdTQfL6AvUihY6.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CXiUyQikfTfoauZh.medium)

### 第 8 步

De-solder the shutter button board from the battery enclosure.
In case you are new to soldering,  here is [https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/How+To+Solder+and+Desolder+Connections/750|Ifixit's soldering guide]
3rd party sellers have the battery housing with the shutter button board already attached. If you have purchased this, begin reassembly.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vGg2T6utQcSdwApN.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ulcNtMh1qxUUQwqJ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TgCA3YrNJwPR3XRp.medium)

### 第 9 步

Using [product|IF145-020|tweezers], carefully remove the shutter button board the rest of the way from the battery housing.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/V1CFBVcTPCxaROlM.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
