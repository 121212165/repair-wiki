title: "Nikon D90 Rear Display Buttons Replacement"

> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #32718](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Nikon+D90+Rear+Display+Buttons+Replacement/32718) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：30 - 40 minutes · 品类：Nikon D90

With this guide you will be able to replace any of the buttons around the rear LCD screen. Buttons get worn and connections break. This guide will help you disassemble the camera so you can replace the buttons. Be careful not to strip the screws when disassembling the camera.

## 需要的工具

- Tweezers
- Phillips #00 Screwdriver
- iFixit Opening Tool

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Removing Casing Screws

Locate the screws on both sides of the camera.
Remove the four 6.3 mm Phillips screws using a PH00 screwdriver.
Be careful not to strip the screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/w1SCdCERef6fg5CI.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UvLgboOepvxHCWSj.medium)

### 第 2 步：Removing the Rear Casing

Use a plastic opening tool to separate the rear case from the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cPy66ekjMn2Of1xX.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wQnruyvkuYQuHplU.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BjgkPfb2X2QyqD4R.medium)

### 第 3 步：Disconnecting the Ribbon Cables

After, remove the two orange ribbon connectors attached to the interior of the camera with [product|IF145-020|tweezers].
Make sure to remove the ribbon cables carefully from the connectors to avoid damaging the camera.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/T2Lst5gmcZRXL54M.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DtDX6rF1dIRWuyfo.medium)

### 第 4 步：Removing the Screws from the Rear Casing

Remove the screws from the back assembly with a Phillips (PH00) head screwdriver.
Remove the ten 5.3 mm Phillips screws.
Remove the three 4.3 mm Phillips screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qrdRRH3YACLovkDp.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/hStRiFaFHaf4IF5l.medium)

### 第 5 步：Removing the LCD and other components

Use a plastic opening tool to separate the LCD and metal plates from the casing.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/eL1WmResMLdkqMXh.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5yyrillQeuX2ZDst.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DfPjmhWXwycSvPbf.medium)

### 第 6 步：Removing the Display Buttons

Use a plastic opening tool to separate the buttons from the casing.
Remove the 4.3 mm Phillips screw from the knob using a PH00 screwdriver.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RJjrWwVYqRIwhrb1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IYOAsCpDXaGoncUi.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/16HWNLVttZgxaaRW.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
