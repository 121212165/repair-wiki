> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #109808](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/iMac+Intel+24-Inch+EMC+2267+CPU+Replacement/109808) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：1 hour · 品类：iMac Intel 24" EMC 2267

Use this guide to replace the CPU on your iMac Intel 24" EMC 2267.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #0 Screwdriver
- TR8 Torx Security Screwdriver
- 6-in-1 Screwdriver
- Heavy-Duty Suction Cups (Pair)
- TR10 Torx Security Screwdriver
- T6 Torx Screwdriver
- Spudger
- Phillips #1 Screwdriver
- Phillips #2 Screwdriver

## 需要的配件

- Pâte thermique

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Lay your iMac front side down on a table with the lower edge facing yourself.
Loosen the single Phillips screw in the center of the access door.
This screw is captive in the access door.
Remove the access door from your iMac.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/eZ2mmfZFNXICRO5o.medium)

### 第 2 步

The glass panel is fixed onto the front bezel with fourteen magnets around its perimeter.
Stick two suction cups to opposing corners of the glass panel.
To attach the [product|IF145-023|suction cups] we sell, first position the suction cup with the movable handle parallel to the face of the glass panel. While lightly holding the suction cup against the glass, raise the movable handle until it is parallel with the other handle.
If your suction cups refuse to stick, try cleaning both the glass panel and the suction cup with a mild solvent.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/p6KIDV5hIdqMQvBL.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TZmRFeiLyRrvNDne.medium)

### 第 3 步

Gently pull the glass panel straight up off the iMac.
The glass panel has several positioning pins around its perimeter.  To avoid shearing these pins off the glass panel, be sure to only pull straight up during removal.
Be meticulous about cleaning the LCD and the inside face of the glass panel before reinstallation, as any fingerprints or dust trapped inside will be annoyingly visible when the display is on.
When replacing the glass, be sure there's nothing between the glass and the frame. Stray cables could be damaged or crack the glass.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3RAYpU3kCCqrGrBr.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the following 12 screws securing the front bezel to the rear case:
Eight 13 mm T8 Torx.
Four 25 mm T8 Torx.
On the 24" iMac Intel Model A1225, the bottom center two screws are long (26mm), four on sides (two left, two right) are medium (18mm), and the remaining 6 (four top, two bottom corners) are short (14mm).

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/aY5eEd5cGTLMJxqX.medium)

### 第 5 步

The front bezel is still attached to the iMac by the microphone cable.
Gently lift the front bezel from its top edge off the rear case.
Once the top edge of the front bezel has cleared the rear case, rotate the front bezel toward the stand and lift it off the rear case.
Rotate the front bezel away from the rest of the device and lay it above the top edge of the iMac.
When reinstalling the front bezel, start at the lower edge and make sure it is flush with the rear case before lowering the top edge onto the iMac.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DeBo1wxAFCN1aO1m.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Z5GW1SNRpnXJLsaa.medium)

### 第 6 步

Disconnect the microphone cable connector, removing tape as necessary.
For the front bezel to sit properly, be sure to tuck the microphone cable and connector into the void next to the camera board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kUpsfj2YCtMpZeBC.medium)

### 第 7 步

Remove the two 5.3 mm T6 torx screws from the LCD connector.
Firmly grasp the pull tab on top of the connector and pull it straight up out of its port.
Make sure that you are pulling the tab and not the connector cable.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IpXMeyHnDxLxjpXE.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Bhif5vbafdDyIlxv.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/p6aD1H6vUcvSAjpK.medium)

### 第 8 步

Remove the eight T8 Torx screws securing the display panel to the rear case.
The iMac shown is a 20" model, but the screw locations should be approximately correct.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/a2EIOaPPLXLjINg2.medium)

### 第 9 步

Place your hands on either side of the bottom of the display panel, and lift it up enough that you can reach the connectors inside.
Do not try to remove the display panel completely as it is still connected to the rest of the device.
While holding the display panel up with one hand, locate and remove the display thermal sensor cable from its connector.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RmJxjqZjhDSeZOSc.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xmaOEbYcdWUj22CA.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DMfQ3IVyx5BeyLoO.medium)

### 第 10 步

While still holding the display panel up, use two fingers to firmly push down on the power supply cable connector from its socket.
Be very careful not to touch the exposed face of the power supply to avoid electric shock from the many large capacitors attached to the other side of the board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FSoFRWcWCgSdXko3.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jo33BpDU643EJbev.medium)

### 第 11 步

Lift and remove the display panel from the device.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/isnfQAuPSQTrZiAn.medium)

### 第 12 步：Logic board teardown

Start by following the excellent [guide|13765|guide] from [https://fr.ifixit.com/User/589955/Jeff+Dickson|Jeff Dickson] from step 2 to step 9 (logic board teardown).

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Sl1dabFjC6GUdkfk.medium)

### 第 13 步：Unplug the temperature sensor

Remove the warranty stickers.
Unplug the CPU temperature sensor.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BRHeGSalAgNWeAXc.medium)

### 第 14 步：Heat sink disassembly

Unscrew the four radiator screws using the Phillips #2 screwdriver.
Flip over the logic board.
Finish unscrewing and remove the four Torx 8 screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/N3rkODDhAodH4vvj.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ksexPFWfYawhpqpw.medium)

### 第 15 步：Heat sink cleanup

Clean the excess thermal paste using a clean cloth and an alcohol solution.
Keep away from the logic board and other electronic parts during the cleaning step.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/LEVLjoY2pPkfXDMu.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DMaRqEt4kx26YKET.medium)

### 第 16 步：CPU disassembly

Use a flathead screwdriver to rotate the CPU locking screw by half a turn.
The locking mount should slightly move backward.
Gently remove the CPU by pulling it up vertically.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jsZBN5xLuutZwImh.medium)

### 第 17 步：Installing the new CPU

Align the socket and the new CPU with the help of the trimmed corner.
Insert the new CPU vertically into its socket.
Do not use any force here, it should fit easily in its socket.
Lock the CPU by rotating the locking screw half a turn clockwise.
Drop a little bit of thermal paste on the center of the CPU.
When reassembling the heat sink, gently squeeze it to spread the thermal paste.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xynjU42evZtkUIOr.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/c6xSpYJDWXWLrgXg.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow the steps in reverse order.
