## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Access door Step 1

o    Loosen the three Phillips screws that secure the access door to the bottom edge of your iMac.
o    These screws will remain attached to the access door.
Remove the access door from your iMac.
o    During reassembly, loosely tighten the access door and align it with the compartment cutout in the iMac's outer casing before fully tightening the screws.
