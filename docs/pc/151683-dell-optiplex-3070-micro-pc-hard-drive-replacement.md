This guide will help you to replace your internal hard drive for Dell OptiPlex 3070 Micro PC.

Scenarios where you need to replace the hard drive:

* If the hard-drive is corrupted and your PC no longer boots
* If you need to more space and wants to upgrade for more GB
* If your PC is running slow - replacing from HDD to latest compatible SSD will improve performance
Follow the step-by-step procedure to replace the hard drive.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #00 Screwdriver

## 需要的配件

- Crucial 2.5 inch SSD

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Prior to beginning this repair, power off the PC and remove all the cables, (including the power cable), to avoid damaging your device.
Use the Phillips #00 screwdriver to remove the single screw securing the case.
Slide the case horizontally away from the screw.
Remove the back case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/COVOWyHRuENpOQbv.medium)

### 第 2 步

Unlock and remove the hard drive from the connector.
Push the two tabs on the hard drive caddy and slide it away from the connector.
Once unlocked, remove the hard drive  from the main board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JVl3jVZKcKvbBM4w.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GMLiwDQHQOSbPJ1k.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ICilpZyyCrcGHDGT.medium)

### 第 3 步

Pull the case away from the four screws at each corner of the old hard drive to remove it from the case.
The hard drive should come out easily and you don't need screwdriver to remove it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HN1NqwTDN6LjiuBF.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XptAjKHRV66nQCIC.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/AAyKXhgn6AeoLqOJ.medium)

### 第 4 步

Put your new hard drive in the case but match the SATA connection to the lines in the hard drive case so you know it is placed correctly.
Lock the four screws from case into the new hard drive.
No need to use a screwdriver as these screws are just a press and lock type.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HvOISYCvWKKYEXK6.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BgYGJTnmg3Vp2BVV.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/NLIVPjbusSWSZKnD.medium)

### 第 5 步

Flip the hard drive upside down.
Insert the hard drive into the connector.
Push the hard drive to lock it in.
You will hear a "click" sound when it locks.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qtRJmFKXPYBMPul1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ORCmPXBeadyASXbH.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/4v6n5nCtVcDCEvLg.medium)

### 第 6 步

Put the cover back on and slide it with the steel tab towards the screw.
Make sure it is securely locked in. Check all the sides for any gaps.
Use the Phillips #00 screwdriver to replace the single screw and securely lock the cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OlEC1pT1dPGHB52L.medium)
