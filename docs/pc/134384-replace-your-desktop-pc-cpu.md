title: "Replace your Desktop PC CPU"

> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #134384](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Replace+your+Desktop+PC+CPU/134384) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：3 - 20 minutes · 品类：Desktop PC

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Open the PC

First, release the screws from the housing.
After that, unplug the cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VXELcDTwlfYnHr2t.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TkSJKkG5yPCrBMas.medium)

### 第 2 步：Remove the cable

Then, you must pull out the hard drive cables.
And the cables from the motherboard as well.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QeyyXbVIntwXlsVU.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XiUoLSu5kZDZqJGK.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iwqBJdjABCjMwCSH.medium)

### 第 3 步：Remove the CPU cooler

Now you need to remove the CPU cooler.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lrBXfuQDqxLFggmH.medium)

### 第 4 步：Removing the CPU

First, you need to remove the thermal paste with paper.
Now, the CPU is removed by lifting a small lever next to the CPU.
Now, the CPU must be lifted out of the socket; to do this, you must be careful and lift the CPU from both sides.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XoWrBNDMVLJTtZ2O.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/E5KWRhPES5F2iBmT.medium)

### 第 5 步：Install a new CPU

Now you must install the CPU.
And apply thermal paste.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xWThIItEIcbBciKA.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/d43bPAMQqSOEOG1t.medium)

## 完成

Follow these steps in reverse order to reassemble your device.
