> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #6284](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/iMac+Intel+21.5-Inch+EMC+2389+Hard+Drive+Replacement/6284) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：40 minutes - 2 hours · 品类：iMac Intel 21.5" EMC 2389

Use this guide to replace or upgrade your hard drive.

This guide also includes steps to upgrade your iMac's hard drive with a SSD. It describes how to install the SSD's temperature sensor so that the Mac's fans will operate at the correct speed.

'''Before beginning any work on your iMac: '''Unplug the computer and press and hold the power button for ten seconds to discharge the power supply's capacitors.

'''Be very careful''' not to touch the capacitor leads or any exposed solder joints on the back of the power supply.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #1 Screwdriver
- Heavy-Duty Suction Cups (Pair)
- Spudger
- TR10 Torx Security Screwdriver
- TR8 Torx Security Screwdriver

## 需要的配件

- Universal Drive Adapter
- 1 TB 7200 RPM 3.5" Hard Drive
- 2 TB SATA Desktop Hard Drive
- 3 TB SATA Desktop Hard Drive
- 4 TB 3.5" Hard Drive
- 2 TB SSD Hybrid 3.5" Hard Drive
- 1 TB SSD Hybrid 3.5" Hard Drive
- 4 TB SSD Hybrid 3.5" Hard Drive
- iMac Intel 21.5" and 27" (Late 2009-Mid 2010) SSD Temperature Sensor
- iFixit 2.5" to 3.5" SSD/HDD Adapter

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Before beginning, unplug your iMac and lay it on a soft surface as shown.
Stick a suction cup near each of the two top corners of the glass panel.
To attach the [product|IF145-023|suction cups] we sell, first position the suction cup with the movable handle parallel to the face of the glass panel. While lightly holding the suction cup against the glass, raise the movable handle until it is parallel with the other handle.
If your suction cups refuse to stick, try cleaning both the glass panel and the suction cup with a mild solvent.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/S6gQ2HIHlQuHu5Sy.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/EucNhvfCJRsBRgOY.medium)

### 第 2 步

Gently lift the glass panel perpendicular to the face of the LCD enough to clear the steel mounting pins attached along the underside of the top edge of the glass panel.
Pull the glass panel away from the lower edge of the iMac and carefully set it aside.
During reinstallation, be sure to meticulously clean the inside of the glass panel and the face of the LCD as any dust or fingerprints trapped inside will be annoyingly visible when the machine is turned on.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dEfYFtAGakgNBgPI.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TRYClBF1GnTXMyiU.medium)

### 第 3 步

Remove the eight 8 mm T10 Torx screws securing the display to the outer case.
The last two pictures are detail shots of each side of the display.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FsUjQtJgaeqcNgE1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/txKZRpCYCaCgrtPs.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IZhsMLKJnWRLXJNs.medium)

### 第 4 步

Slightly lift the top edge of the display out of the outer case.
Do not lift it too much. There are several cables attaching the display to the logic board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JJrCWMp1gZN1UT4k.medium)

### 第 5 步

Pull the vertical sync cable connector out of its socket on the LED driver board near the top left corner of your iMac.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/snjtGbXS21FTSBMO.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xdAUxEVSgd5dQSHy.medium)

### 第 6 步

Rotate the display out of the outer case enough to disconnect the LED backlight power cable from the LED driver board.
Disconnect the LED backlight power cable by depressing the locking mechanism while pulling the connector away from its socket (toward the bottom edge of the iMac).

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iMBFDuJiaisDPeFC.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5yr44v46WOxDbGkx.medium)

### 第 7 步

The display data cable plug has a wire lock.  Disengage the plug's lock by rotating the plastic tab so that it points upward.  (It is not a pull tab.)
Pull the display data cable straight out of its socket on the logic board.
Do not lift up on the display data cable, as its socket is very fragile. Pull the cable parallel to the face of the logic board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/v1k3LXRGKXpV6j6l.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dcjmwaavKSaIn1pO.medium)

### 第 8 步

Disconnect the LCD thermal sensor cable connector from its socket on the logic board.
If your fan is spinning full speed after completion, check this connection or the hard drive's thermal sensor cable.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BTiYLEbf35GsJli1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TfFCJGU561MPwbxJ.medium)

### 第 9 步

Carefully pull the display toward the top edge of your iMac and lift it out of the outer case, minding any cables that may get caught.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QcxFhTVxIJaFC3PF.medium)

### 第 10 步

Pull the hard drive temperature sensor connector toward the top edge of the iMac to disconnect it from its socket on the logic board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ev5pWjqCwpLZqQ6o.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YYLQKISgYTZrUMsn.medium)

### 第 11 步

Remove the two 9 mm T10 Torx screws securing the upper hard drive bracket to the outer case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/mVfMPLrTPr26tO5c.medium)

### 第 12 步

Slightly rotate the hard drive out of the outer case and lift it up off its mounting pins toward the top edge of the iMac.
Be careful not to contact the AirPort card as you lift the hard drive out of its recess.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kIMcrfWvQINlO5mu.medium)

### 第 13 步

Disconnect the SATA data cable and SATA power connector from their respective sockets by pulling each connector away from the hard drive.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5WIVjQlewZCQoxVX.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YBkTrJmJIwQJMaDE.medium)

### 第 14 步

Before disconnecting the thermal sensor cable, take note of its orientation. It is extremely important to reinstall the connector in the current orientation so the fans will not run at full speed. If you have multiple pins on your replacement hard drive, put the connector closest to the SATA connections and closest to the PCB board.
Disconnect the hard drive thermal sensor cable from the hard drive.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/uo5O5NJPCoqZxEh1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lMZlQXDHDLObgJIq.medium)

### 第 15 步

Remove the two T8 Torx screws securing the upper bracket to the hard drive.
Remove the upper bracket from the hard drive.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iaFlRIK13WN4yS66.medium)

### 第 16 步

Remove the two T8 Torx pins from the other side of the hard drive.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/WNdAfjP2qInpZsku.medium)

### 第 17 步

Carefully peel off the piece of EMI foam attached to the front of the hard drive.
Don't forget to transfer this to your new hard drive.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3Sgl3JbAIQVFoOAn.medium)

### 第 18 步：Replace the hard drive with an SSD

Follow the next steps if you are replacing your hard drive with an SSD kit.
If your SSD enclosure looks like the first image, [https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/iMac+2009+2010+SSD+Replacement/139093|follow this guide instead|new_window=true].
Line up the SSD connectors with the metal enclosure's sockets.
Press the drive against the enclosure sockets until the SSD is firmly seated.
The SSD connects with the enclosure in only one direction. If the connectors don't line up, rotate the SSD and try again.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HKQbp12ZISQhNfAv.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/C535jLFwdCXbrulv.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wDWxsjpZCDD5vCjk.medium)

### 第 19 步

Use a Phillips screwdriver to install the four silver screws (two on each side) along the edges of the SSD to secure it to the enclosure.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FtBOnUuDYZJTjvey.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RvVXsqFVNCWuybvj.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UmyFUVECjaDZNRjp.medium)

### 第 20 步

Plug the included sensor-enabled SATA power cable into the enclosure's port.
The cable is keyed to connect in only one orientation. If you can't connect the cable, rotate it 180° and try again.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HU4PFgCWjYNkrIi4.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gWGSmdLLDiJOASYW.medium)

### 第 21 步

Peel the white liner off the back of the small temperature sensor board in order to expose the adhesive.
Adhere the temperature sensor board to an exposed, metal surface of the SSD, close to the SATA connector.
Fold the excess temperature sensor wires so that they are out of the way while you install the enclosure.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/nFPHEiNHZQZIIWir.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/25p6UPH1X2Hpm1ut.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kZJrQ2FKFMAo4eGZ.medium)

### 第 22 步

Use a T8 driver to install the mounting pins from the old hard drive onto the sides of the enclosure.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TB3g2BfZthZmgZof.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UZXpLNW61PfcoLXO.medium)

### 第 23 步

Attach any mounting brackets removed from the old hard drive onto the enclosure.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Aq2vsCxhf2S3qufG.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6HieMRWYI2Pgc2Yn.medium)

### 第 24 步

Connect the iMac's SATA power cable to the other end of the new sensor-enabled SATA cable.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ciVrfTP4LtNtlEV3.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QDKoTyhGQj1clwQP.medium)

### 第 25 步

Connect the iMac's SATA data cable to the enclosure's socket.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CWAiJZexabkmRKlw.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UXFeucvE5hqbSPEU.medium)

### 第 26 步

The images used in this step come from a 2017 iMac. Your cables may look slightly different, but the procedure remains the same.
Slide the SSD enclosure into the space previously occupied by the hard drive.
Route the SATA cables so that they will not interfere with any other components.
Try to keep the cables free from any sharp bends, and route them such that they are not pinched.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YJqasivdZRKDWMjy.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FI6EqbMtmSvAcIM6.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DpsoNu5h3obcGm6g.medium)

### 第 27 步

If your motherboard has a socket for a hard drive temperature sensor, follow this step to plug the sensor wires in.
Find the connector on the motherboard labeled '''HD TMP''' or '''HDD TEMP'''.
If you have trouble locating it, trace the two-wire temperature cable you disconnected from the old hard drive.
If the previous temperature cable is still connected to the board here, disconnect and remove it.  You will no longer need it.
Connect the temperature sensor's two-wire red-black cable to the motherboard plug labeled '''HD TMP''' or '''HDD TEMP'''.
The connector is keyed to fit in only one orientation. If it doesn't fit one way, rotate it 180° and try again.
Route the excess wire so it does not interfere with any other components.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dcPea2s6XDCrdU2s.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/KELmZTXyNHo1VNrd.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
