One day, my Fire TV Stick remote control stopped working, and at the same time, the batteries in the compartment had leaked.... In this guide, you will learn how to disassemble the remote control far enough to access the battery contacts so you can remove, clean, and reinstall them.

## 需要的工具

- Schraubenzieher Kreuzschlitz
- Soldering Iron
- Flathead 3/32" or 2.5 mm Screwdriver

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Opening the remote control

First, pry off the ring of the D-Pad. This is easiest to do by sliding a flat and sturdy tool under the inner part, pressing down slightly on the center, and getting under the ring. A dull, thin knife or a flat card, for example, could be suitable as a tool.
Now, remove both screws using a Phillips screwdriver. You should now have two screws, a small cover for the center, and the outer ring.
Finally, use the same flat tool to widen the gap on the side between the top and bottom part of the remote control: you just need a little force to release the snap-fit connections.

### 第 2 步：The interior of the remote control / Cleaning

The PCB can now almost be removed. First, simply remove the rubber cover for the buttons. Now you need a soldering iron to release and remove a single solder joint at the bottom socket.
The bottom battery contact should now be removable as a single part from the plastic casing.
In my case, I measured an electrical resistance of 12 Ohm with my multimeter, which was the electrical contact with the spring. After cleaning, this value dropped to 3 Ohm. (These are not exact resistance values, of course, but qualitatively it was recognizable that the contact had improved!)
To clean it, I simply washed off the corroded spots with some vinegar and warm water and dried them. There was no dirt on the PCB in my case; I would also not recommend washing it under water.

### 第 3 步：Assembling the remote control

Reconnect the contact part to the PCB. Otherwise, simply follow all steps in reverse.
Please note: the center part and the ring of the D-Pad must be aligned correctly during assembly. The orientation can be seen from the plastic parts on the underside of the ring and center part.

## 完成

Follow the steps in reverse order to reassemble your device.
