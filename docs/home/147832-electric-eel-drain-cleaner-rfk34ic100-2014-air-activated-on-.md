## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Remove 4 Phillips head screws holding on the cover plate and gasket behind it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/unnNZ5tXHmCgEBJI.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/f5MfWPyBTRQH5sCE.medium)

### 第 2 步

Remove bottom 9/16 nut that holds the pneumatic tube on the switch.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iFEIMp6Bp3jNfPcs.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VWOyWceWTMb1UiIc.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/KyUTWWt3GT3NZDBn.medium)

### 第 3 步

Thread the new switch into the pneumatic tube, careful threads are plastic.
Attach yellow wire to the top and the black wire to bottom of switch with disconnect.
Tighten 1/2 nut on the pneumatic tube until snug.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/srKF2qP3qCInAdfm.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dRwLIHRp3HjTvRbI.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
