Use this guide to replace the power cable in a General Pipe Cleaners Drain Cleaner PMEBO 2016.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #2 Screwdriver
- 9/16" Deep Socket
- Socket Wrench
- 6-in-1 Screwdriver
- Needle Nose Pliers

## 需要的配件

- General Pipe Cleaners Reverse Switch ME-610-GR
- General Pipe Cleaners Ground Fault With Cord 136000

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Before you begin, make sure the drain cleaner is disconnected from power.
Use a Phillips #2 driver to remove the two screws securing the control box cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SKAPK61UEcnXWWnk.medium)

### 第 2 步

Remove the control box cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZGRopeTVnyWlIyJB.medium)

### 第 3 步

Use a 9/16 inch deep socket and ratchet to remove the nut securing the reverse switch.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YdUjPbvtExssNDSh.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the reverse switch guard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/aHMMQw3RtVWLvRJL.medium)

### 第 5 步

Squeeze the tabs on either side of the reverse switch connector.
Pull the two halves of the connector apart to disconnect it.
Take note of where everything is connected in the control box before disconnecting anything else.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/fpXDYVVXpSCr6vJs.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jZ1FwaYGFfJEcyFF.medium)

### 第 6 步

Pull the white cable straight off of its spade connector on the power switch diaphragm.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CDQYDDwCVG5xhSuV.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/blvH6qkmyRHXBSCT.medium)

### 第 7 步

Pull the black cable straight off of its spade connector on the power switch diaphragm.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/mh5QARJvw3jNvJYk.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lreTPWuGCYCOPvCy.medium)

### 第 8 步

Slide the switch assembly down out of its hole in the control box and pull it out of the control box.
Be careful not to snag the switch on any cables during removal.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YomLE4RggWy4SwIV.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6jgNgbIe1pxku5eA.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/fa3PaP2CBIOrCs5U.medium)

### 第 9 步

Push in on the small tab on the side of the plastic power cable clip.
While holding the tab down, pull straight down on the power cable.
Do not remove the cable yet, it is still connected to the drain cleaner. Slide it down just enough to move the clip out of the control box.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pAKw6gIXfCoxMPUV.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/O1lTFEYqeHZcFnBb.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/h6GHMTcRdm1MWrZp.medium)

### 第 10 步

Use a Phillips #2 screwdriver to loosen the two screws securing the power box cover.
With the screws loose, slide the bottom of the cover to the right enough so the whole cover can swing down and away from the box opening.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/fHdlfMh3tiIRo1MF.medium)

### 第 11 步

Use a flathead screwdriver to remove the green screw securing the green power cable wire.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sZgLygCGiZusII6j.medium)

### 第 12 步

Use pliers to pull the white power cable straight off of its spade connector.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3xH5uTCpeTnNnaxl.medium)

### 第 13 步

Feed the green power cable wire through the control box first, then carefully pull down on the entire power cable and remove it.
Make sure you don't snag the power cable on any wires or on the control box during removal.
During reassembly, feed the white wire through the control box and into the power box first, then the green wire.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RWO3ZTsWYkYgikFC.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RNiVdbZ4TJO5OnlV.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
