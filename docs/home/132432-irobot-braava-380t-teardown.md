> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #132432](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/iRobot+Braava+380t+Teardown/132432) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：30 minutes · 品类：iRobot Braava 380t

This guide shows how to open an iRobot Braava 380t and access its motors and wheels to clean or disassemble them. After lifting the cover, disconnect its connector before removing it completely.

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Flip over the Braava
Remove the 4 screws circled in red in the photo.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SrZE3tDlPQZOUnJT.medium)

### 第 2 步

Flip over the Bravva
Raise the cover, which should offer no resistance.
Disconnect the red and black wire plug.
Remove the cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/erTwAgfgBWTtJt2X.medium)

### 第 3 步

The robot is now open. Take the opportunity to clean the contactors on the front.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/mQEfqHZwkGKPekT1.medium)

### 第 4 步

To continue dismantling and gaining access to the motors, remove the stickers.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lZPFWUQ3Tp2Zsnli.medium)

### 第 5 步

The screws of the engine covers are then clearly visible.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/eL5yoqWprm4vF2RK.medium)

### 第 6 步

Remove the covers.
Do not try to pull the engine out at this time!

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PmFxFQlyRNvHSSVO.medium)

### 第 7 步

Remove the wheel cover by turning it a few degrees.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GkLBWOysMRygVHFK.medium)

### 第 8 步

Remove the circlip using special pliers (cut here to be small enough)
Take out the wheels, and remove all the stuck hair and dirt.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TcTb56NXaHNCU5FZ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/I4fUk6ItXr2xMAni.medium)

### 第 9 步

Undo the 2 screws. Next, remove the wheel guard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PUqdoi4mPCB42r2B.medium)

### 第 10 步

You can then take out the motor and possibly dismantle the gearbox. But it's no use, as I didn't find any hair inside.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ttatWFTZHNvjC5ai.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pTqTcNMEQPxEgVKF.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow the instructions in reverse order.
