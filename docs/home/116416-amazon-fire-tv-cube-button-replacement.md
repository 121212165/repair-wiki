> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #116416](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Amazon+Fire+TV+Cube+Button++Replacement/116416) · 难度：**困难** · 耗时：1 hour · 品类：Amazon Fire TV Cube

This guide will instruct and explain how to remove and replace the buttons on your Amazon Fire TV Cube. This task requires a spudger and delicacy when disconnecting wires. If you follow the steps provided, you will successfully be able to replace the Cube buttons.

## 需要的工具

- Metal Spudger
- TR10 Torx Security Screwdriver
- Magnetic Project Mat
- Spudger
- T6 Torx Screwdriver
- Tweezers

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Using a metal spudger,  push down between the edge seals on the base and pry off the base.
Do not confuse the top thin layer of rubber with the thick palette that needs to be removed.
This may require substantial force.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JbGiZgxlCWJO5cZS.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FOICIeRAFAEj61rN.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/LdTFkj3UIHNONwga.medium)

### 第 2 步

Use the T-10 bit from the iFixit tool kit to unscrew the four 1.4 inch corner screws on top of the first layer.
There are 4 washers to go with each of the screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RIqQPDAHEdYZPqsw.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/WU4MvJMuxc4Og4aX.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/AKQOCD4DNGEOlsxJ.medium)

### 第 3 步

Lift and remove the full frame.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gvOplpZJAZAoZ1iL.medium)

### 第 4 步

Lift and remove the speaker.
Carefully match the outlets on the speaker with the outlets on the motherboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vormYIKWVm1TUSEK.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1SEVBTSbsaUNjSdf.medium)

### 第 5 步

Lift the black latch by gently prying up with the flat end of the Spudger.
Detach the copper tap by pulling it out.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GBEqwl5EYtwRXu31.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/WqRWCdkNZoac1ZTI.medium)

### 第 6 步

Use the pointed end of the Spudger to carefully detach the black and white wires from the motherboard.
Make sure all three attachments (the copper tab and both wires) are fully disconnected before continuing.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vuD6x1GVBIqIT5HN.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/rfS1lX6lpmnuQEn2.medium)

### 第 7 步

Gently pry the motherboard up and off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/AFB4ZtNDXBcpZU3Y.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Wuw2SCBlVrxNxKRs.medium)

### 第 8 步

Detach the wires from the opposite end of the frame using the flat end of the Spudger.
You can’t see where the wires are attached because it is hidden underneath the white frame.
Make sure both wire attachments are fully disconnected before continuing.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jE6mECfL1shQRHRb.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pTiPt5vXyQrDRxGI.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Ut4WH5LYeiTYBY1U.medium)

### 第 9 步

Pry apart the black plastic face from metal frame.
This requires substantial force.
Be careful not to tear connecting copper tab.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CMR3SluhQPEMShja.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/U4fH3F42IGfOvIZU.medium)

### 第 10 步

Lift the black latch to detach the copper tab by gently prying with the [product|IF145-020-5|angled Tweezers].
Use the Angled Tweezers to carefully pull out the copper tab.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Ty1TfcJbDX5yKuHE.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bYlTFtmPX5jAdP2S.medium)

### 第 11 步

Unscrew the  five T-6 quarter inch screws.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tPjT4hZsxnquNZbb.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GUPYu2TC2MKAUs36.medium)

### 第 12 步

Lift and remove white the frame to reveal the circuit board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lhtbdoNgElcEEFpp.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QN6wDhH5TyqUuiBd.medium)

### 第 13 步

Using a the Metal Spudger, pry up the circuit board to reveal a rubber button panel.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/faRcRhO6jNlnnPXF.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ORFyuVajLvO1egFp.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HLejXvWEJFdbihHC.medium)

### 第 14 步

Peel back the rubber button casing.
Pay close attention to the orientation of the rubber casing.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vCemeWWGNQcFwEaW.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/LmohuE2bgHPhIYlb.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
