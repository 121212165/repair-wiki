Use this guide to replace a broken toilet flapper chain in your toilet.

The toilet chain is the thing that connects the handle to the toilet flapper. When you push the handle, the chain moves, causing the flapper to lift as part of the flushing process. As a toilet ages, rust can sometimes build up on the flapper chain. Over time, this rust weakens the chain, and one flush may cause it to break.

Before starting, check to ensure the handle is rotating smoothly and that you are holding it all the way down (or up, depending on the handle) during flushing. Doing this will help save time in determining whether an improper flushing technique is being used, whether the handle needs repair, or if a chain replacement is actually necessary.

## 需要的工具

- Mini Locking Pliers

## 需要的配件

- Toilet Flapper Chain

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Lift the toilet lid off the toilet and set it aside.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Cyrc6FQURkQDvRBW.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/I1ZtSuxZyCYuRFBP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/eBLKJXwHglFCmg4D.medium)

### 第 2 步

Turn the water supply valve to your toilet clockwise until it's fully closed.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lKuqLlJNrsLJgFwa.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QaFXHh5AdTLRcZAR.medium)

### 第 3 步

Push the flush handle down (or up, depending on the toilet) to flush the water out.
Keep holding the handle until the flush is complete and the tank is empty.
You may need to directly lift the flapper if pushing the handle down does not work.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FTbhZXVa6eeDURxL.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GUru2poKqiwXAiCE.medium)

### 第 4 步

Unhook the chain from the trip lever arm.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qUADJoXntlqfEmQt.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kSJIl4ENbZ1HmymF.medium)

### 第 5 步

Unclip each side from the mounting post on the flush valve to remove the flapper.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VHCxrM2x1mQkNQJW.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gWkICSD5pJCFNwb3.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XZVXU3dmaUCICOvM.medium)

### 第 6 步

Pull on the hooks securing the chain and slide the old, broken chain off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/K4l4YcAnCE23veEq.medium)

### 第 7 步

Slide one end of the new chain onto one of the flapper's hooks, and close the hooks up by pushing them in.
If your new chain is too long, you may need to use some pliers to cut the chain to a length similar to the old chain to prevent slack (and a bad flush!).

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iGyNcuNScqR2lCY2.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
