This guide covers how to fix a MOEN Chateau Single Handle Bathtub. This is an older model, but is similar to newer versions in terms of steps required.

This guide begins with the handle removed and covers the installation process.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #2 Screwdriver

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Attach the brass stem into the wall (if not already installed).
Make sure your water faucet is not leaking. If it is, call a professional.
Turn off the water in your house, as failing to do so could lead to leaks.
Close the drain to prevent any parts from falling in.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2ZUWhhW12oPOQr1Y.medium)

### 第 2 步

Attach the brass sleeve to the faucet.
Make sure it is perfectly aligned with the hole in the wall, then tighten it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Ht4gRyaGhdUYTClQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3QBRhjwkuhxGaJso.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/yZa5IkaOLGrgMlPI.medium)

### 第 3 步

Attach the plastic adapter to the brass sleeve.
Make sure it is properly aligned with the brass sleeve.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gLUQIh2B4yAqbVci.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/msWLRkimVQkpvICw.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/uFldk3QNQFFlLxHx.medium)

### 第 4 步

Attach the plastic ring to the plastic adapter.
Ensure they are securely attached and properly in place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vPaYqdyEFR3p1LxV.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/KAxGQGDUQ2jKZohX.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3bgvG5OZOHyBDBQN.medium)

### 第 5 步

Attach the cartridge and the second brass stem to the faucet.
Align the cartridge and ensure it is securely in place. It should not move; make sure to tighten.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/51LWgPOEMYWjV2xA.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2rFTYT6Z2fk6xSiT.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/13AEPp63xwq5CEyj.medium)

### 第 6 步

Attach the handle using the screw.
I used a round-head screw approximately 90 mm in length. The length may vary depending on your model.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pRtrHsoM4mYL2lnw.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/WrqoBU1PAKBMlDYQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/aVsCEbUoPKqKjH36.medium)

### 第 7 步

Use a screwdriver compatible with a round-head screw to tighten it.
Test the handle to ensure the screw is securely tightened and the handle turns properly.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SL4RJGwVFketMYyK.medium)

### 第 8 步

Test the faucet to ensure it works properly and check that there are no leaks.
Mine worked perfectly and feels brand new!
