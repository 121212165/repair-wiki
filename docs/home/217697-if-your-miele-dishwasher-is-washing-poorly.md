> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #217697](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/If+your+Miele+dishwasher+is+washing+poorly.../217697) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：No estimate · 品类：Miele Dishwasher

If your Miele dishwasher, which is over ten years old, is washing poorly despite you having tried ten different detergents and polluted half the world with cleaners that turned out to be perfectly useless, before you throw it away with a broken heart given that it looks brand new (considering what it costs!), know that with a little handiness, you will most likely be able to get it back into perfect working order at zero cost. In areas like my city where the water is very hard (meaning it is rich in limescale), limescale deposits not only on the heating coil but also inside what is called, for example, the "water pocket." This component, which is located on the side of almost all dishwashers and is made of semi-transparent plastic, contains various circuits and other gizmos inside it, which the wash water, drawn from an opening with a filter on the side of the loading compartment, passes through, activating solenoid valves and switches with various functions necessary for the proper functioning of the appliance. In my case, after removing the housing, I noticed that many parts of this water pocket were clogged with limescale, so I thought that this could be the cause of the dishwasher's problems. I then disassembled the water pocket and put it in the shower (not having a bathtub), where I filled it with a 50/50 solution of water and muriatic acid, leaving it to work overnight. The next morning, the incrustations were all gone, so I disposed of the acidic liquid and rinsed the pocket with cold water. I reassembled everything and, magically, the dishwasher started working again like new. Try it to believe it! I believe this procedure that I applied to my old Miele can work with machines of any other brand. In conclusion: if the water in your city is full of limescale, this could be the cause of your dishwasher's malfunction.
