We will deal with a fairly common problem: error 26 on an iRobot from the i series.

I found several tutorials on YouTube concerning this problem; the solution I am offering you definitively resolved my problem.

You can also, if you wish, simply replace the suction unit by purchasing it from iRobot; the repair will be shorter, but will cost around 100€.

Using the solution at the end of this guide will cost nothing, or almost nothing! So... give it a try!

## 需要的工具

- Kreuzschlitzschraubendreher PH 2
- Silicone Lubricant Spray

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Removing the bottom part

Let's start by removing the side brush using a Phillips screwdriver. (red arrow)
Next we remove the five screws that hold the lower bracket using a Phillips screwdriver. (red arrows)
You can now remove the lower base

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BNMDPsiTuTBaq2UF.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/S1lVCtr5Qyy1bP5x.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gQblADSxCTNnNKgM.medium)

### 第 2 步：Removing the suction unit

Now unscrew the 4 screws holding the suction unit.  (red arrows)
You can remove the complete suction unit 'upward'.
***If you want to just replace the suction unit***, you can replace it. You will need to search online for "cleaning head module", this module is no longer sold by Roomba, pay attention to the part numbers! Once you have received the part, follow the guide in reverse to reassemble it.
***If you are a little more handy, you can continue the guide to solve your problem at low cost! ***

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/V1w4XFLZeICPvmrL.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/EDTNfDWQ3yo1hXxM.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/k2AYH6bYTFeRaVHP.medium)

### 第 3 步：Removing the Vacuum Motor

You can unscrew the four screws (red arrows) from the motor support.
I suggest you to free the harness a bit so you have more room to work.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iw1KWutxV1MuLDBT.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CxnPeEIjkMGMgdhi.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/V6CQCUViRJl1JMFp.medium)

### 第 4 步：Removing the Impeller from the Motor

We now need to remove the impeller on the motor shaft.
***Work slowly, take your time!***
You can use a spatula to help you remove the impeller while turning it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Eo4asXQr4qDkmkrK.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jONHcSqTrCksZaIe.medium)

### 第 5 步：Disassemble the impeller/motor casing

You can now remove the two screws that hold the engine to the impeller housing. (bilateral red arrows)
Be careful, make sure that the 'washers' do not get lost in your home. (central arrow)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JoVrIMlKmc3bmArv.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/WZWFXuIoVub4APUQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/nY5kHdZjFpV14cuW.medium)

### 第 6 步：Removing the rotor

You can now remove the rotor (axle) from the stator (copper part)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/N4as6lF6hQGFjQx4.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dlBhWIhCErwvTCRI.medium)

### 第 7 步：Lubricate the rotor

You now need to grease the rotor (axle) so that there is less friction with the stator.
You must use silicone spray or semi-solid silicone grease.
You can now reassemble your axle to the stator: the problem should be solved.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5Q3waGI3pACqSFPC.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PpI3O2WbuLqMeYoO.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow the instructions in reverse order.
