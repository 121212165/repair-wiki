> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #239692](https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/Electrolux+ELFW7537AW+washing+machine+teardown/239692) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：1 - 2 hours · 品类：Electrolux ELFW washing machine

This guide will guide you through tearing down your washing machine pretty thoroughly.

I will probably make a troubleshooting guide so that you don’t need to take the machine apart completely.

## 需要的工具

- Philips #2 Screwdriver
- Square #1 Screwdriver
- Phillips #1 Screwdriver

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Prerequisites!

Please! Do not skip, if you do this will probably not end well!
Make sure there’s no clothes in the machine*
Turn off all water
Drain the machine with diagnostic mode 11
Unplug the machine
*If your clothes are trapped because you can’t open the door, do step 1 then skip to step 3-F.2 then go back to the start of step 3.

### 第 2 步：Top cover

With a #2 Robertson remove the two screws at the top on the back

### 第 3 步：Top of front panel screws

Unscrew the #2 robertsons with rubber washers

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3O3wJhWEyNQhJ16n.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/EOQGHbVFSgZsTpIw.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/raj1lAfNSM4Sexy1.medium)

### 第 4 步：Soap drawer

Remove soap drawer, push on the “push” button and pull the drawer out
Remove the 2 #2 Robertsons and the #1 Philips

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5lnAXFKyrdLl6YCM.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/oduliGIRZggPA2Kf.medium)

### 第 5 步：Bottom front panel screws

Next remove two #2 cross from the bottom left and another two from the bottom right on the front.
You’re going to have to feel around for them they’re in pairs close together.
Can’t take a picture of them, sorry, you’ll get over it.

### 第 6 步：Emergency manual drum door opening

Try opening the washer drum door.
If you can’t and ***++you are sure++*** that it is safe to do so, you can press the release leaver on the door lock.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2M6iOntmZyNrChoO.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Euh1MaZ4NxVKyqgc.medium)

### 第 7 步：Door glass removal

Optional step, I would recommend it if you’re going to do troubleshooting.
This step should be done with a friend!
Remove the 9 #2 Robertsons. Then ***++slowly++*** close the door halfway, one person pulls the chromed plastic ring out. The second person holds the glass as it will become unseated and could fall if it is not held.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XAdYIMMRjFiQQdQW.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vQNDEUtiglbXurPt.medium)

### 第 8 步：Drum rubber gasket removal

Pull back the rubber at the top of the drum gasket, it will reveal a spring pull on it then peal it out of its groove and pull the rubber off the metal lip on the front panel.
To reinstall, once the rubber is over the metal lip, put the metal ring at the 6o clock position on the drum then place it expanding from that first bottom middle position up to the top of the circle.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CMYe4UyqnYS2RwlW.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/36xcWDWGZNCVntvt.medium)

### 第 9 步：Front panel assembly removal

Lift up a bit pull forward then pull up completely on the front panel.
This movement in two steps is to dislodge the front control panel trim from the inside of the soap drawer then getting the front off it’s four pins with 8 shaped holes in the front panel that hold it on.
Disconnect the two cables connected to the front panel.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/AMpIZT4aS5ZvZlNB.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1rVSWLYbqhJH4HOG.medium)

### 第 10 步：Back panel

The numbering on this step is mostly for reassembly but the screws are again #2 Robertsons.
I’m your case you should find a motor behind this panel but, this machine is in repair.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PylwiXRLPdxqEZ5l.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gIocXTeWgZ4QHdnp.medium)

### 第 11 步：Dismounting the main control board

Please be careful with this step, and do other troubleshooting steps first since this board manages high voltage and has capacitors that could cause severe harm or death so give them the space to the forbidden trash cans!
To dismount the box containing the control board there’s one #2 square screw holding it to the side of the machine
