This guide explains how to repair a Navien NPE-240A tankless water heater when the display shows error code ***E110***. This code indicates an issue with the air intake vent, which may be caused by a blockage or a damaged air intake collar.

This guide demonstrates how to clear any obstructions and replace the air intake collar.

***Note:*** Before you begin, check the display screen for ***Error Code E110*** to ensure this is the right guide for your tankless water heater.

***Caution:*** Turn off the unit’s power and shut off the gas supply. Failing to do so can cause injury or damage.

## 需要的工具

- Impact Driver
- 5/16" Nut Driver
- Power Drill

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

To ensure this is the correct guide for you, check the display screen for ++***Error Code E110***++.
Turn off the power to the unit and shut off the gas supply.
Forgetting to turn off power to the unit and shut off the gas supply can result in possible damage or injury.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HUTxXyAv3E45Qkr3.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/C24gubo3t4PZoiCQ.medium)

### 第 2 步

Use an impact driver with a 5/16" nut driver to loosen the #6 machine nut screw from the clamp.
Loosen the clamp and disconnect the pipe and vent collars from the water heater to access the air intake filter.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JQKYsJttYKm6dYBd.medium)

### 第 3 步

Inspect the air intake filter and vent pipes for dirt or obstructions.
Clear any debris by hand if necessary.
If the ++***E110 code***++ remains after cleaning, prepare to replace the faulty air intake vent collar.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/H4M1iudEMRxfjnWP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/EcXaHPJyyGpUGg2B.medium)

### 第 4 步

Use an impact driver with a Phillips head screwdriver bit to remove the four 1/2" machine screws that connect the damaged air intake vent collar to the water heater.
Remove the damaged air intake vent collar.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/H6qemYAarUKu1C6w.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sWAS2nqKNCpN4fjZ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vWoSuloQNuktGBeT.medium)

### 第 5 步

Install the new air intake vent collar.
Secure the new vent collar with the four 1/2" Phillips machine screws using the impact driver with a screwdriver bit.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ypCLEtfsgusV35FA.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gLZmfr2xKwnMAvJO.medium)

### 第 6 步

Use the impact driver with a 5/16" nut driver to reconnect the pipe and vent collars to the water heater.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ve5tvSRLFKPjg2ot.medium)

### 第 7 步

Turn on the power to the unit and open the gas supply.
Power on the heater to confirm that the ++***E110 error code***++ no longer appears.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/eIlDJLomqmAwXiQv.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HCOlqBhdToGi4nRB.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wVxPZriuP2QVZwSb.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
