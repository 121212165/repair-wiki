This guide demonstrates how to safely access the internal components of the Whirlpool 980 Series Dishwasher for cleaning, troubleshooting, and minor maintenance. It is designed for users who want to inspect parts such as the pump, spray arms, filters, door seals, and wiring harnesses. Proper technique is critical to avoid damage or water leaks.

== ***Tools Required*** ==
* Phillips #1 screwdriver
* Flathead screwdriver
* Nut driver set (1/4" and 5/16")
* Pliers
* Towels or a small container for water drainage
* Soft brush or cloth
== ***Step 1 — Power & Water Safety*** ==
# Turn off the dishwasher and unplug it from the electrical outlet.
# Shut off the water supply at the valve under the sink.
# Remove the bottom dish rack and any loose items from inside.
== ***Step 2 — Remove the Toe Panel*** ==
# Use a Phillips screwdriver or nut driver to remove the screws securing the toe panel at the bottom front.
# Carefully pull the panel away to access the pump and internal connections.
== ***Step 3 — Disconnect the Door Panel (Optional for Deeper Access)*** ==
# Open the door fully and locate screws along the inner edge of the door.
# Remove screws to release the outer door panel.
# Lift gently and disconnect any ribbon cables connecting the control board to the door panel.
== ***Step 4 — Inspect the Pump and Motor Assembly*** ==
# Locate the main pump assembly at the bottom of the dishwasher.
# Check for debris, broken impellers, or loose fittings.
# If necessary, remove mounting screws to lift the pump for cleaning or replacement.
== ***Step 5 — Check Filters and Spray Arms*** ==
# Remove the filters from the bottom of the tub. Clean thoroughly with a soft brush.
# Inspect spray arms for clogs or cracks. Remove and rinse as needed.
# Ensure no debris is lodged in the spray arm bearings or mounting points.
== ***Step 6 — Inspect Wiring & Connections*** ==
# Visually inspect wiring harnesses for frayed or loose connections.
# Check the float switch for proper movement and alignment.
# Use pliers to reseat connectors if necessary.
== ***Step 7 — Reassemble Dishwasher*** ==
# Reattach the door panel, reconnecting any ribbon cables.
# Replace the toe panel and tighten all screws.
# Restore the water supply and plug in the dishwasher.
# Run a short test cycle to ensure everything functions properly.
