### 第 3 步：Arranging Bottle Jack

Start by placing the bottle jack ontop of the metal with the corner being on lip of the structur.
After its set on the lip line the middle/top of the bottle jack with the bolt of the snake motor mount

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1MteVgii4Av1ZuIY.medium)

### 第 4 步：Time to lift

Adjust the rode of the bottle jack up till the rods head and the bolt have a nice firm and connection
Do Not: Over crank the rod with too much torque.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QktnI2scXX56lMGj.medium)

### 第 5 步：Unscrewing the Screw Hex Point

Using the 5/32 Head on a alen wrench, unscrew till the head of the screw is loose.
Tip: Use the bar of the bottle jack as a breaker bar for more leverage.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JGU5tGI1Alq1qQ6H.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/X3DgCP1ECsCWNFFR.medium)

### 第 6 步：Taking Off The Hex Nut

Using a 1/2" wrench, take off the Hex nut on the Screw hex point.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZkTxymsobPLImJkn.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Y44nhuMbRbmkqcKt.medium)

### 第 7 步：Raising the Motor Assembly

Crank the bottle jack up and feel for tention.
When your at the desired tention. Plug the snake into an outlet and flip the switch too ON.
WARNING: Be aware of the cage and wire from the snake. Failure to do so could result in injury, Please be safe when unit is on!
Squeez the petal too test if unit is at the desired hight and tention
Unit should have just enough tention not be able too stop the cage from spinning.
Note: Place your foot or hand against the cage with a fair amount of force. If you are able too stop the cage from spinning, the unit will need more adjustment on the belt.
When finished Retighten the hex screw and bolt on the shaft shown on step 5 and 6.
Finally remove pump jack from the unit
