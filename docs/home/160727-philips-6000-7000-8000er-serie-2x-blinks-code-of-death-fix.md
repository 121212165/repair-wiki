Do you have this problem with a Philips TV from this series? It suddenly doesn't turn on after a crash and flashes twice? Here's the solution!

***Explanation:***

''As expected, this is a serial defect. It occurs in these models due to insufficient cooling, as the built-in porcelain cooler isn't designed to withstand the heat generated by the TV's CPU. As a result, cold solder joints form over time, which then begin to crumble. Because the motherboard no longer has contact with the CPU, it can't be turned on.''

***Execution:***

The connection between the CPU and the motherboard needs to be restored. This is best done by heating the CPU, as the solder joints will melt and reconnect. So, place the motherboard in an oven or heat it with a heat gun.

***''Info:''***

***''If the TV turns on but there is no picture or the picture is blurry, check all cables connected to the motherboard.''***

## 需要的工具

- Schraubenzieher Kreuzschlitz

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Remove the back panel

Loosen all screws on the back panel and carefully remove it.
Set the back panel aside.

### 第 2 步：Disconnect cables

Disconnect all cables from the mainboard.
Take photos to facilitate reconnection. (And feel free to add them here!)

### 第 3 步：Loosen the motherboard screws

Remove all screws holding the motherboard.
Carefully remove the mainboard.

### 第 4 步：Preparation

Place the motherboard on a heat-resistant surface.
Remove all plastic components.

### 第 5 步：Oven procedure:

Preheat oven to 200°C - 230°C (top and bottom heating).
Place aluminum foil on the baking tray
Place the motherboard on the aluminum foil and heat it in the oven for about 5-8 minutes.
Then place on a flat surface and let it cool to room temperature for about 10 minutes

### 第 6 步：Procedure with the hot air gun:

Set the hot air gun to medium heat (230°C-300°C)
Position the hot air gun just above the CPU
Heat the CPU evenly, for about 5 minutes.
Then let it cool to room temperature for 10 minutes

### 第 7 步：Insert mainboard

Put the mainboard in its place.
Tighten all retaining screws.

### 第 8 步：Connect cables

Reconnect all cables as shown in the previous photos.
Check that all connections are tight and secure.

### 第 9 步：Attach the back panel

Attach the back panel and tighten all screws.

## 完成

After you've baked the special motherboard MC style, you can reinstall the motherboard in the TV and start it up. Then you can check whether the TV is working properly and isn't displaying any error messages.
