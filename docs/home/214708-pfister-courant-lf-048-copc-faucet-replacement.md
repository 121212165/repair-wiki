This guide will show you how to replace your old faucet for the Pfister Courant 2-Handle 4 in. Centerset Bathroom Faucet (LF-048-COPC). Over time, faucets start to leak, and mineral buildup can cause water flow to be reduced. Eventually, it can start to rust where it can leave stains around the faucet. Preventing this is ideal, since the area will be more cleaner, hygienic, and can maintain water efficiency. Installing the Pfister Courant (LF-048-COPC) will help with reducing water consumption output from the usual 2.2 gallons per minute to 1.2 gallons per minute.

Prior to starting, make sure to turn off water valve(s) to prevent excess water from spewing out and making a mess of the surrounding area.

## 需要的工具

- Adjustable Wrench
- Channellock Groove Joint Pliers
- Shop Towel
- Microfiber Cleaning Cloth
- Small Bucket
- Teflon tape

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Turn off the water valves by turning them clockwise.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2dkXb1J66ahGQDZ2.medium)

### 第 2 步

Turn the adjustable wrench counterclockwise to loosen the water supply lines.
Make sure to use either a towel or small bucket for any water that comes out to prevent a mess.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TTyIeuOx2meFWgFy.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vLqGQigjF3glVJIE.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XQXlQ2iH2epONqNf.medium)

### 第 3 步

Turn the mounted nuts counterclockwise to loosen.
Use an adjustable wrench if the mounted nuts are too tight to remove by hand.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xaRlvTfSryyL3YlQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/o2YNkTUEVPUHjYNl.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BvCIYCNUTktj3dud.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the old faucet.
Gently lift the old faucet up when removing to avoid damage around the sink.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/oDfaXHMDbUJnnhxT.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/o35XuOFyQOB2es6n.medium)

### 第 5 步

Wipe off the residue left behind with a damp cloth.
Dry the area off with a paper towel.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iOPhaPNfJfCYWr4p.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/quAeT4esESicTvFy.medium)

### 第 6 步

Clean around the faucet to clear off any gunk.
Apply Teflon tape to both shanks.
Wrap the Teflon tape three to five times around both shanks clockwise.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/NnJiBWCi15qLxQNm.medium)

### 第 7 步

Align the three-hole base gasket with the existing mounting holes.
Line up the faucet with the corresponding holes and slide it in carefully.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3BsLGtIRE64LKUCP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PxSJqrCUdbBwHCBw.medium)

### 第 8 步

Screw on the mounting nuts clockwise to secure the shanks on the faucet.
Use an adjustable wrench if the mounting nuts need to be tightened more.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tVbD55PaJYhlkl6b.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/N1TPcnNGYqTR4gWQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wqDa6KLrj4WUXx2j.medium)

### 第 9 步

Reconnect the water supply lines by screwing them clockwise using an adjustable wrench.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ljD4xGMShAYbCDwo.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ecPuQZbVoESKXX5H.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/utLnNlnYifBUrHeo.medium)

### 第 10 步

Turn on the water valves by turning them counterclockwise.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/pq3gZNFfvwMuiWOI.medium)

### 第 11 步

Test out the faucet and check if there are any leaks.
Let the water run for 30-60 seconds to ensure water flow is clear and is free of rust or debris for personal use.
