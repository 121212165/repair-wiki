As you know Iphone batteries could drain very fast if i you don't care it so,Replacing an iPhone battery can help bring an older phone back to life when the battery no longer lasts as long as it used to. In this guide, I’ll show the process step by step, including opening the phone, removing the old battery, and installing the replacement. Take your time and work carefully to avoid damaging any components.

Steps to add

Step 1 — Power off the iPhone

Turn the iPhone completely off before starting the repair.

Step 2 — Remove the bottom screws

Remove the two screws near the charging port and keep them somewhere safe.

Step 3 — Open the display

Carefully separate the display from the frame using proper phone-repair tools. Don't force it.

Step 4 — Disconnect the battery

Open the internal shield/bracket and disconnect the battery connector.

Step 5 — Remove the old battery

Carefully release the battery adhesive and remove the battery without bending, puncturing, or damaging it.

Step 6 — Install the replacement battery

Place the replacement battery in the same position and secure it properly.

Step 7 — Reconnect everything

Reconnect the battery and display connectors, then reinstall the protective brackets.

Step 8 — Close the iPhone

Carefully place the display back into the frame and reinstall the bottom screws.

Step 9 — Test the phone

Turn the iPhone on and check the display, touch, charging, speakers, cameras, and battery status.

⚠️ Safety note: If the battery is swollen, damaged, or hot, don't continue the repair—have an adult or qualified repair technician handle it.
