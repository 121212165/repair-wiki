Follow this guide to eject and remove the SIM card tray in your Google Pixel 7a.

## 需要的工具

- SIM Card Eject Tool

## 需要的配件

- Google Pixel 7a SIM Card Tray - Genuine

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Remove the SIM card tray

Insert a SIM eject tool, bit, or straightened paper clip into the SIM card tray hole.
Press the SIM eject tool into the hole to eject the SIM card tray.
Remove the SIM card tray.
