The expectations for this new mid-range smartphone are huge, after all the predecessor—[[Topic:Samsung Galaxy A50|Galaxy A50|new_window=true]]—was one of the [https://www.theverge.com/2020/2/28/21157386/iphone-best-selling-phone-worldwide-xr-11-samsung-a-series-counterpoint-research|most successful smartphones] Samsung released in 2019. Can the successor keep up? A teardown might be the only way to find out!

The specs may not impress at first sight, but there's quite a lot here for the price:

48 MP ƒ2.0 main camera, 12 MP ƒ2.2 ultra wide camera, 5 MP ƒ2.4 macro camera for close-ups and a 5 MP ƒ2.2 depth camera for multiple live focus effects; 32 MP ƒ2.2 front-facing camera
Exynos 9611, octa-core (4×2.3GHz Cortex-A73, 4×1.7GHz Cortex-A53)
Built-in storage: 64GB/4GB RAM, 128GB/4GB RAM, 128GB/6GB RAM
Android 10.0
Fast wired charging with 15W
USB-C and a nearly extinct 3.5 mm headphone jack

### 第 2 步

The back cover shines like glass, but it's a ruse! It's plastic—perhaps a cost-saving measure? Whatever the reason, we're happy to see it. This plastic makes the A51 less shatter-prone than [https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/Samsung+Galaxy+S20+Ultra+Teardown/131607|the glass-backed Galaxy S phones|new_window=true].
Plenty of heat from our [https://www.ifixit.com/Store/Tools/iOpener/IF145-198?o=8|iOpener|new_window=true] softens the adhesive and we can start battling our way in.
And we come through it unscathed! The back side lifts off cleanly (with a little gentle force). No booby-trap cables here.

### 第 4 步

Samsung sends the A51 into the selfie-race  with a 32 MP ƒ2.2 front camera. That’s a 7 MP increase from its predecessor—quite instagrammable.
For comparison: The [https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/iPhone+11+Pro+Max+Teardown/126000#s243403|iPhone 11 Pro Max’s|new_window=true] rear and selfie cameras provide 12 MP each while the [https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/Samsung+Galaxy+Note10++5G+Teardown/125590#s242795|Samsung Galaxy Note 10+|new_window=true] offers 16 MP/12 MP for the rear cameras and 10 MP on the front. ### 第 5 步

Just like its predecessor, the A51 also features an optical in-display fingerprint scanner which is placed near the daughterboard.
Optical fingerprint sensors work by illuminating the finger and taking an image of the bright and dark areas representing the ridges and valleys of the fingerprint. ([https://www.ifixit.com/News/14385/how-ultrasonic-fingerprint-sensors-work|More on fingerprint sensor technology.|new_window=true])
The daughterboard comes out without a fuss and brings the USB-C plug and the beloved 3.5 mm audio jack to light.
A dedicated audio jack reduces wear and tear on the USB-C port, which is nice. Both ports are soldered in place though, so replacing one individually will require soldering skills.
That said, this configuration is better than the [https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/Samsung+Galaxy+S10+and+S10e+Teardown/120331#s232002|single-board Galaxy S10|new_window=true]! At least this tiny daughterboard can be swapped out with relative ease should one or both ports fail on you.

### 第 9 步

In line with other recent Samsung phones, the battery squats in a little pit of glue-lined sadness with no pull tabs, but we quickly set to work digging it free with heat and a plastic card for some leverage.
The A51 features a Li-ion 4000 mAh battery, the same as the A50!
Now that the battery is out, we can compare it to its competitors! Fully topped off, this 3.85 V, 4000 mAh cell will deliver up to 15.4 Wh of power. That’s more than the [https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/iPhone+11+Pro+Max+Teardown/126000#s243570|iPhone 11 Pro Max|new_window=true] with 15.04 Wh and only slightly less than the 15.79 Wh battery in the [https://www.ifixit.com/Teardown/Samsung+Galaxy+S10++Teardown/120436|S10+|new_window=true].

### 第 10 步

With the phone empty we turn to the screen... and it puts up a real fight!
After an initial round of heating, we didn't get very far with our [https://www.ifixit.com/Store/Tools/iFixit-Opening-Picks-Set-of-6/IF145-123?o=2|Opening Picks|new_window=true] before we heard that dreaded noise...
'''''Crack!'''''
Like all Samsung phones, the A51's screen is strongly glued to the frame. There's a reason that Samsung sells screen replacements pre-adhered to brand new frames, and this is it!
The good news is once you crack it, you can't go back! The rest of our removal procedure is infinitely less stressful. We use a [https://www.ifixit.com/Store/Tools/Plastic-Cards/IF145-101?o=1|Plastic Card|new_window=true] to carve away the rest of the screen.
Once we finally break it free, we find the culprits: two thick adhesive strips that cover a large area of the screen.

### 第 12 步：Final Thoughts

The Samsung Galaxy A51 earns a '''4 out of 10''' on our repairability scale (10 is the easiest to repair):
A single Phillips driver takes care of all the screws.
Many components are modular and can be replaced independently.
The plastic back cover is glued in place, but can be removed fairly easily with heat.
The battery is accessible but heavily glued in place.
Screen repairs require a lot of disassembly while battling tough adhesive.
