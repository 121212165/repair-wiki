Follow this guide to remove the SIM card tray in your Google Pixel 8a.

## 需要的工具

- SIM Card Eject Tool

## 需要的配件

- Google Pixel 8a SIM Card Tray - Genuine

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Remove the SIM card tray

Firmly press a SIM eject tool, bit, or straightened paper clip into the SIM card tray hole on the left edge of your phone until the tray ejects.
Remove the SIM card tray.
