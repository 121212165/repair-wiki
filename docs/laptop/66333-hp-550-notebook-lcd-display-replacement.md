> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #66333](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/HP+550+Notebook+LCD+Display+Replacement/66333) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：1 hour · 品类：HP 550 notebook

Follow this guide to fix your LCD display.

## 需要的工具

- T15 Torx Screwdriver
- Cacciavite con testa a croce n. 1

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Remove the battery

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/L1sTCnPa4BRXPK2K.medium)

### 第 2 步

Remove the 8 pads and the underlying screws

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/H3xdm3jJH54GJZLo.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bGIHRGjpvDxIdKD3.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3kTfUtmSUvghViMv.medium)

### 第 3 步

Use a thin tool to separate the lcd frame
Unhook the cover on the 2 hinges on the upper side

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YYw5HyDKoIV1QUtc.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lxjyxmNUbNlQJgtn.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/AgfgNj1jlxqCLR61.medium)

### 第 4 步

Remove the LCD cover with delicacy, paying attention to the wires and removing any adhesive if present.
Remove 4 lcd fixing screws on one side and 4 screws on the other
Force the back cover slightly to access the last screw on both sides

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/LTunUZ5NvGf2UXSG.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JWbp5DfYgcDSTwLA.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QgqnF2ObaJZ5jTZv.medium)

### 第 5 步

Disconnect the lcd panel connector
Gently lay the LCD panel on the keyboard.
Disconnect video display connector.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jfMnoIFJeXSGqVLN.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/STnKChhNStucqwKF.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tA4hRO4OrtZnDiRE.medium)

### 第 6 步

Remove the LCD panel and replace with the new one

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JCpTcbWUfdWFN3rB.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble the device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
