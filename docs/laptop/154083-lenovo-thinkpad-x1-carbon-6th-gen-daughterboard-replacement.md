## 需要的工具

- Phillips #0 Screwdriver
- iFixit Opening Tool

## 需要的配件

- Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon 6th Gen Audio Daughterboard and Cable

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Use a Phillips #0 screwdriver to unscrew the five screws that secure the back cover to the laptop.
The screws do not completely come off from the back cover. Loosen until the screws are completely loose.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/NFtYMIq2pIVe1NLS.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/f3HPZLtAIooGCgUh.medium)

### 第 2 步

Use a Jimmy to lift the cover directly up off of the locating pins.
Though a  metal opening tool is shown in the image, we recommend using plastic opening tools to minimize any cosmetic damage.
Remove the back cover.
If you encounter resistance when lifting the cover, revisit the screws to see if any of them are still threaded on to the device.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HKMFZVKrjam3PWWH.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6ZP1DMLSvgYbwIhX.medium)

### 第 3 步

Use the Phillips #0 screwdriver to remove the four 5 mm screws that secure the battery to the laptop frame.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2xBAZNGktDaK54Lm.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IIjEKPgWLiLkttCO.medium)

### 第 4 步

Lift the battery from the bottom edge and gently slide it out of the connector on the motherboard to remove it from the laptop.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OBGNfvQoRcUkViXt.medium)

### 第 5 步

Carefully remove cord from daughterboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/mx5Oo4ArkK5GZZhS.medium)

### 第 6 步

Use a PH0 screwdriver to remove the three 2mm screws holding the daughterboard in place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vEAcGWT51BDsGyvE.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
