Use this guide to replace the hard drive on the HP Elitebook 1040 G4. The solid-state drive (SSD) is a storage component of the computer which and saves programs. It is a long term storage device and may need to be replaced or removed. There are typically 3 common reasons you would need to remove a hard drive: (1) if the hard drive fails, (2) need more storage, or (3) need a speed boost. Before beginning, be sure to completely power off your computer.

To complete this guide, you will need a #T5 Torx head screwdriver and a Phillips #00 screwdriver.

## 需要的工具

- T5 Torx Screwdriver
- Phillips #00 Screwdriver

## 需要的配件

- HP EliteBook 1040 G4 SSD

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Use a T5 Torx screwdriver to remove the eight 5 mm screws securing the back cover to the chassis.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OwRelP1Q2kdGOtoE.medium)

### 第 2 步

Lift the back cover up and off the device.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6TZHI6LLpl1SrHDP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Xt1FHAqjNTIryfDn.medium)

### 第 3 步

Using the Phillips #00 screwdriver, remove the single 5 mm screw securing the SSD to the motherboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3qcafAvj1Z2MUDrk.medium)

### 第 4 步

Lift the SSD up and out of the device.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bqVImxNQHw4odgtD.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
