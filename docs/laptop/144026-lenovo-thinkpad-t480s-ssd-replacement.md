Use this guide to replace the SSD in your Lenovo ThinkPad T480s laptop.

An SSD (short for “solid-state drive”) is a storage component in a computer. SSDs use flash memory, as opposed to the spinning disks and moveable parts used in an HDD (hard disk drive). As a result, SSDs are much quieter and operate more quickly than HDDs. Signs your SSD may be faulty include corrupted data, read-only errors, frequent crashes, or [link|https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_screen_of_death|blue screen of death].

If you are able to recover any data saved to your SSD, be sure to back up your files before beginning this guide. Also, make sure to [link|https://support.lenovo.com/eg/en/solutions/ht510660-removal-and-replacement-videos-thinkpad-t480s-20l7-20l8|disable the internal battery,] power down your device, and disconnect from any external power sources before you begin.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #1 Screwdriver
- iFixit Opening Tool

## 需要的配件

- Crucial P3 NVMe PCIe M.2 2280SS SSD
- Crucial P3 Plus NVMe PCIe M.2 2280SS SSD
- Crucial P5 Plus PCIe M.2 2280SS SSD

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

[link|https://support.lenovo.com/eg/en/solutions/ht510660-removal-and-replacement-videos-thinkpad-t480s-20l7-20l8|Disable the battery], power down the device, and unplug the power adapter from your device before proceeding/beginning the guide.
Loosen the six captive screws with a Phillips #1 screwdriver.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RNt3cWxTIjeD1Mhe.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/haCRyLGBRClsXGrF.medium)

### 第 2 步

Remove the bottom cover with an opening tool.
Start from the top edge near the hinge and work your way along the edge until all of the clips are unsnapped.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Vboy5of3qMKgumu1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Xg36ebBqZsGYEUXf.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lIwLqVaU1qnjeJil.medium)

### 第 3 步

Lift the bottom cover off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/l4tenwgnMuEBiPRu.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OndiEfoacVynMaGD.medium)

### 第 4 步

Use the Phillips #1 screwdriver to remove four 4.5mm screws that hold the battery down to the mid frame.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/rMuXJjsAEqTwJ6yk.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/keHTFFHcBqAGFHH5.medium)

### 第 5 步

Lift the battery near the connector and remove it from the system.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qxlZs34IiTCm3AB1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RhPWGMiEmmS2NAxQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/WdXBnRMenO53EqKo.medium)

### 第 6 步

Use the Phillips #1 screwdriver to remove one 2.8 mm screw securing the SSD.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/wv6e4ZkyZWWwXrOw.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/nWTF2SXDTrTpGRGZ.medium)

### 第 7 步

Pull the SSD out of the socket.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tlKRwV6BdUU6ATen.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TYWs6PXQlgEsNkvY.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/mhYPL4vvMZ2FcEhv.medium)
