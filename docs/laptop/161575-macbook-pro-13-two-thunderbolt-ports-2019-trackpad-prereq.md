Trackpad prerequisite without the reassembly bullet.

## 需要的工具

- P5 Pentalobe Screwdriver Retina MacBook Pro and Air
- Suction Handle
- iFixit Opening Picks (Set of 6)
- Tweezers
- Spudger
- T5 Torx Screwdriver
- iOpener

## 需要的配件

- MacBook Pro 13" (A1706, A1708, A1989, A2159) Trackpad
- MacBook Pro 13" Retina (A1708, A2159, A2289) Trackpad Cable

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Remove the trackpad

Use a T5 Torx driver to remove the two 1.9 mm screws securing the trackpad cable connector bracket.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/U2b3A2wsIwDEJSge.medium)

### 第 2 步

Remove the trackpad cable connector bracket.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Yur6NX12KReGeRQD.medium)

### 第 3 步

Use a spudger to disconnect the trackpad ribbon cable by gently prying its connector straight up from the logic board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZR2IDdxWMfCngk5R.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/43gd5FYWjqBlN3rO.medium)

### 第 4 步

[https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/iOpener+Heating/25705|Prepare an iOpener|new_window=true] and lay it on top of the trackpad ribbon cable for about a minute, in order to soften the adhesive securing the trackpad ribbon cable to the top of the battery.
If you don't have an iOpener, use a hair dryer to warm up the cable instead. The cable should be warm, but not too hot to touch. Be careful not to overheat the battery.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jSlPDRqIu56Jvt1e.medium)

### 第 5 步

Carefully peel the trackpad ribbon cable up off the battery, and push it out of the way.
Do not fold or tear the cable.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GicrFqeqDWCDtNso.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/MxYTA2EBx5JKB5F6.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VqOUGQ6uFgHCkxnc.medium)

### 第 6 步

Use a T5 Torx driver to remove the ten screws securing the trackpad assembly:
Two 4.3 mm screws
Eight 5.8 mm screws

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/LSFSSLWCda6X6H6m.medium)

### 第 7 步

Remove the trackpad assembly.
You'll need to swing the display open slightly to remove the trackpad.
Take care to feed the trackpad ribbon cable through the MacBook Pro's frame without snagging.
Be sure not to lose the six small washers (two circular and four rectangular) resting on the underside of the trackpad.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/abmUpIlg1JRgBQQ1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tTNowZG4GVoxPfVJ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/4RAOs3sPHNVROfvA.medium)

## 完成

Compare your new replacement part to the original part—you may need to transfer remaining components or remove adhesive backings from the new part before installing.

***To reassemble your device, follow the above steps in reverse order.***
