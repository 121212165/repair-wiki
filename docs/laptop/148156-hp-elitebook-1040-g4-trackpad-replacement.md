> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #148156](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/HP+EliteBook+1040+G4+Trackpad+Replacement/148156) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：15 - 25 minutes · 品类：HP EliteBook 1040 G4

Use this guide to replace the trackpad of the HP EliteBook 1040 G4. The trackpad is an essential laptop component used for controlling the cursor without an external mouse. If a drastic decrease in trackpad sensitivity is noticed and/or if the trackpad clicker buttons no longer function properly, then the trackpad should be repaired or replaced.

Before replacing the trackpad, make sure your battery isn't swollen. Swollen batteries can disrupt trackpad function, and can be dangerous. Replace and [link|https://www.ifixit.com/News/11199/how-to-recycle-iphone-battery|properly dispose|new_window=true] of swollen batteries immediately.

To complete this guide, you will need a T5 Torx head screwdriver and a Phillips #00 screwdriver.

Before beginning, be sure to completely power off and unplug your computer.

## 需要的工具

- T5 Torx Screwdriver
- Phillips #00 Screwdriver
- Spudger

## 需要的配件

- HP EliteBook 1040 G4 Touchpad
- HP EliteBook 1040 G4 Touchpad Cable

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Use a T5 Torx screwdriver to remove the eight 5 mm screws securing the back cover to the chassis.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OwRelP1Q2kdGOtoE.medium)

### 第 2 步

Lift the back cover up and off the device.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6TZHI6LLpl1SrHDP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Xt1FHAqjNTIryfDn.medium)

### 第 3 步

Use a Phillips #00 screwdriver to remove the eight 4 mm screws securing the battery to the motherboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dgxgNfq5suqElvUI.medium)

### 第 4 步

Use a plastic spudger to carefully disconnect the battery pin connector from its port, slowly lifting the pin connector from each side until it releases.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2i6dY1TVyi4M2fpd.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1pShETHcrHgnXCnT.medium)

### 第 5 步

Lift and remove the battery from the device.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IPkJNQShsWR1yJTd.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5vH1O4xVNgKRV2j4.medium)

### 第 6 步

Peel back the foam cover to reveal the underside of the trackpad.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/v1tsSgslyRBeE6Xo.medium)

### 第 7 步

Carefully disconnect the white ribbon cable attached to the underside of the trackpad.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Y3QYRfHN4eeSnoj2.medium)

### 第 8 步

Carefully disconnect the black ribbon cable attached to the chassis.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/MT4wQcJJXhyHIUCU.medium)

### 第 9 步

Use a Phillips #00 screwdriver to remove the six 2 mm screws securing the trackpad.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/46C5wk5hWXBpVriY.medium)

### 第 10 步

Lift and remove the trackpad from the device.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/MP6iSPuPSCS5BIjX.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
