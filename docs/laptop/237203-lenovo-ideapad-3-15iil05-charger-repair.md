> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #237203](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Lenovo+IdeaPad+3-15IIL05+Charger+Repair/237203) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：45 minutes · 品类：Lenovo IdeaPad 3-15IIL05

This guide will show how to reconnect the charger cable for the IdeaPad 3-15IIL05 laptop, as it has desoldered from the internal board, resulting in a loss of voltage at the charger tip. Follow these steps to learn how to reconnect your cable.

## 需要的工具

- High Content Rubbing Alcohol
- Toothpick
- Credit Card / Plastic
- Precision Utility Knife
- FixHub Power Series Smart Soldering Iron
- Wire Stripper
- Digital Multimeter

## 需要的配件

- Tapa Superior (Logo Lenovo)
- Tapa Inferior
- Carcasa Disipadora Interna
- Protección Salida de Cable

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Add isopropyl alcohol

Apply isopropyl alcohol to the seams between the top and bottom covers using a toothpick.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/h4G5cMDQUlODhEqn.medium)

### 第 2 步：Separate the top and bottom covers

Apply pressure with the plastic card to separate the top and bottom covers.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PAslOKAEkWpDkQBe.medium)

### 第 3 步：Removing the top cover (Lenovo Logo)

Use a plastic opening tool to pry and remove the top cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/stXdfgmIeH2RHQbB.medium)

### 第 4 步：Remove lower cover

Use the plastic card to apply pressure and remove the lower cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OdC2aS6vsXJpXCZB.medium)

### 第 5 步：Remove internal heat sink cover (Upper)

Once the upper and lower covers have been removed, the heat sink cover will be visible; remove the upper one, which is secured by a small tab.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/25CCfQPh1ADCpjKW.medium)

### 第 6 步：Remove internal heat sink housing (Lower)

Remove the internal heat sink housing (lower) using a soldering iron to remove the solder joints securing it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kjJenchs5oOOULUo.medium)

### 第 7 步：Identifying output cables

Verify which are the output cables; in this case, one has a white sheath and the other is unshielded.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/frh4lWPd6Zy2JPEh.medium)

### 第 8 步：Soldering wires in place

Solder the wires in place using the soldering iron, ensuring the white-insulated wire is placed on the position marked (VOUT) on the board and the uninsulated wire is placed on the position marked (GND).

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qGKpHDOGr2ZSpSLG.medium)

### 第 9 步：Verify voltage at the charger tip after soldering

Using a multimeter set to the DC voltage scale, place the red probe inside the charger tip and the black probe on the outside of the charger tip, with the charger plugged into a power outlet, to verify that we have the operating voltage.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cjqYwxDZ5F4LgHZl.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
