> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #208147](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Lenovo+ThinkPad+T14+Gen+1+Speaker+Replacement/208147) · 难度：**很简单** · 耗时：15 - 20 minutes · 品类：Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 1

Use this guide to disassemble and replace the speakers in a Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 1 laptop. The speakers are responsible for producing audio output, and over time they may become damaged, or stop working altogether.

This guide was photographed using an iPhone 13, which features a dual‑12MP camera system. (Nahom’s Phone Camera)

No special tools or advanced technical skills are required to complete this repair.

Before beginning, make sure the laptop is fully powered off and disconnected from any power source.

## 需要的工具

- Plastic pry tool (recommended)
- Phillips #1 Screwdriver
- Phillips #0 Screwdriver

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Using a Phillips #0 screwdriver, remove the two 3 mm Phillips #0 screws holding the speakers down.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/hHFJSIT6ORkDYwTR.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vKPDDWDu1IpVVhhZ.medium)

### 第 2 步

Follow the cable to where it plugs into the motherboard to locate the speaker connector.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Qt1iRZvQiwLaZhhn.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/uvU2OyQEsrMhHcAJ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/NqaRssiOUwN5GEth.medium)

### 第 3 步

Using a plastic spudger or your fingernail, gently lift the connector straight up from its socket.
Lift speaker module straight up and out of its slot.
Carefully guide the speaker cable out as you remove the module.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ciQDtPgWflPylWwP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZeyfpJZBEQTjBNRH.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/D4aGHdKIiReKYVon.medium)

### 第 4 步

Once the speaker is free, pull the cable out completely.
Set the old speaker assembly aside.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xm4j56seUtoMjMWy.medium)

## 完成
