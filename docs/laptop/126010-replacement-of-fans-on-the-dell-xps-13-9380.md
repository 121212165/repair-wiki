'''''You need to disconnect or replace the cooling fans on your Dell XPS 13 9380, this guide is for you!'''''

## 需要的工具

- Mako Driver Kit - 64 Precision Bits

## 需要的配件

- Dell XPS 13 9370/9380 Fan

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Remove the back panel

Before you begin, be sure to turn off your laptop and unplug it from the outlet.
Use a Torx T5 driver to unscrew the eight screws securing the back panel.
Use a spudger to pry up and loosen the back panel.
Lift the back panel up slightly from the non-hinged edge.
Pull the back panel away from the hinged edge, wiggling it back and forth to loosen the clips.
When the back panel clips are free, remove the back panel.

### 第 2 步：Remove the battery

Disconnect the cable from the battery by pulling the connector towards the battery.
Pull parallel to the battery, not straight up.
Remove the four screws (M2 x 2) securing the battery.
Remove the screw (M1.6 x 4) securing the battery.
Remove the battery from its recess.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xR3HbMEpDMSuWFJT.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HjAByEb4NL4R2IMQ.medium)

### 第 3 步：Remove the fans

***1-*** Remove the screw (M1.6 x 3) that fixes the WiFi card and the cable holder for the webcam on the motherboard.
***2-*** Remove the WiFi card and the cable holder for the webcam from the motherboard.
***3-*** Unplug and lift the webcam cable from the motherboard. You will need to remove the adhesive tape that attaches the cable.
***4-*** Unplug the power cable from the right cooling fan of the motherboard.
***5-*** Unplug the power cable from the left cooling fan of the motherboard.
***6-*** Remove the two screws (M1.6 x 3) that attach the cooling fans to the motherboard.
***7-*** Remove the cooling fans from the motherboard.

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow the instructions in reverse order.
