> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #112877](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/MacBook+Pro+15-Inch+Touch+Bar+Late+2016+Lower+Case+Replacement/112877) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：5 - 10 minutes · 品类：MacBook Pro 15" Touch Bar Late 2016

Use this guide to remove the bottom cover, a.k.a. lower case. This serves as the starting point for every repair on your MacBook Pro.

'''For your safety, discharge your MacBook Pro's battery below 25%''' before you begin this procedure. A charged lithium-ion battery can create a dangerous and uncontrollable fire if accidentally punctured.

## 需要的工具

- P5 Pentalobe Screwdriver Retina MacBook Pro and Air
- Suction Handle
- iFixit Opening Picks (Set of 6)

## 需要的配件

- MacBook Pro 15" Retina (Late 2016-2019) Lower Case
- MacBook Pro (A1706/A1707/A1708/A2141/A2159/A2289) and MacBook Air (A2179/A2337) Plastic Feet

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Disable Auto Boot

Before starting this procedure, you must disable your Mac's '''Auto Boot''' feature. Auto Boot powers on your Mac when you open the lid, and may be accidentally triggered during disassembly. [https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/How+to+Disable+Auto+Boot/110034|Use this guide] or follow the abbreviated instructions below to disable Auto Boot. ''This command may not work on all Macs.''
Power on your Mac and launch '''Terminal'''.
Copy and paste the following command (or type it exactly) into Terminal:
'''sudo nvram AutoBoot=%00'''
Press '''[return]'''. If prompted, enter your administrator password and press  '''[return]''' again. ''Note: Your return key may also be labeled ⏎ or "enter."''
You can now safely power down your Mac and open the bottom case, without it accidentally powering on.
When your repair is complete and your Mac is successfully reassembled, re-enable Auto Boot with the following command:
'''sudo nvram AutoBoot=%03'''

### 第 2 步

Completely power off and unplug your MacBook Pro before you begin.
Close the display and flip the entire laptop upside-down.
Use a P5 Pentalobe driver to remove six screws securing the lower case, of the following lengths:
Four 4.7 mm screws
Two 6.6 mm screws
Note the orientation of the screws as you remove them—they need to be reinstalled at a slight angle.
Throughout this repair, [https://www.ifixit.com/Store/Tools/Magnetic-Project-Mat/IF145-167-4|keep track of each screw|new_window=true] and make sure it goes back exactly where it came from to avoid damaging your device.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/B2dyIeZ13jMyq4oN.medium)

### 第 3 步

Press a suction handle into place near the front edge of the lower case, between the screw holes.
Lift up on the suction handle just enough to open a small gap under the lower case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VZFA1MgVRKCoIZlv.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QWPIB6vDdZCBEspJ.medium)

### 第 4 步

Slide the corner of an opening pick into the gap you just created underneath the lower case.
Slide the opening pick around the nearest corner and then halfway up the side of the MacBook Pro.
This releases the first of the hidden clips securing the lower case. You should feel and hear the clip pop free.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CIMWfGuyrYGngcBl.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/D2IKRLqXgXXTOlRy.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Min2sZEd15jYUULk.medium)

### 第 5 步

Repeat the previous step on the opposite side, using your opening pick to release the second clip.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/6khJa1nYdTCtVhyh.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YPy6HHqhj6xPiCK4.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RiRVxkVMFGVYdUbQ.medium)

### 第 6 步

Lift the front edge of the lower case (the side opposite the display hinge) just enough to slide your fingertips underneath and get a good grip on it.
Two additional hidden clips near the middle of the cover should pop free at this point, if they haven't already.
Don't lift more than an inch or so, and don't try to remove the lower case yet.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SUXHrYyCaAOVvcMN.medium)

### 第 7 步

Insert the flat end of a spudger into the hinge cutout in the lower case, at the corner nearest the headphone jack.
Pry to lever the lower case away from the hinge area, pushing it slightly towards the front edge of the MacBook.
Repeat at the opposite side of the cutout.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Q3wSm3gSIuJhmXMp.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iEDerHHt3vimlLML.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZabeLb1cJMrRXMGS.medium)

### 第 8 步

Mind your fingers or wear protective gloves during this step—the case edges may be very sharp.
Grab the front edge of the lower case again, and pull firmly to slide it towards the front edge of the MacBook (away from the hinge area), separating the last of the clips.
Pull first at one corner, then the other.
Pull to the side—not up.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FJ1XaUFTMZqVZmfu.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/566NJvVaErMxyUHm.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kUdSXd5WqbZIsEkn.medium)

### 第 9 步

Remove the lower case.
To reinstall the lower case:
[https://d3nevzfk7ii3be.cloudfront.net/igi/kUdSXd5WqbZIsEkn.full|Set it in place|new_window=true] and align the sliding clips near the display hinge. Press down and slide the cover toward the hinge. It should stop sliding as the clips engage.
When the sliding clips are fully engaged and the lower case looks correctly aligned, press down firmly on the lower case to engage the four hidden clips. You should feel and hear them click into place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2jJJSwavVeNYqoh1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cViDJDVLBOjuCf1f.medium)

## 完成

'''To reassemble your device, follow the above steps in reverse order.'''
