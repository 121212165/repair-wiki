title: "Replacing Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 1 Ethernet Port"
---

> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #208141](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Replacing+Lenovo+ThinkPad+T14+Gen+1+Ethernet+Port/208141) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：10 - 30 minutes · 品类：Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 1

Use this guide to replace the RJ-45 Ethernet port in the Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 1. A damaged Ethernet port may cause unreliable wired network connections or prevent cables from locking securely. Before beginning, power off the laptop and disconnect the battery to prevent electrical damage.

## 需要的工具

- Plastic pry tool (recommended)
- Phillips #1 Screwdriver
- Phillips #0 Screwdriver
- Spudger
- Tweezers

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Turn off your laptop completely before starting the disassembly process.
Use a Phillips #1 screwdriver to loosen the following captive screws:
The top two 6.8 mm corner screws
Four 5.8 mm-long screws
The screws will remain in the case when fully loosened.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vyLKxkKbhZwnwUE3.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ktJiUIYTZMBbKsVq.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DuCZHy14jOsQPRwG.medium)

### 第 2 步

Use a plastic opening tool to pry the bottom panel.
Insert the end of the opening tool into the seam between the bottom and top cover.
Slide the opening tool around the edge of the bottom panel to release the clips holding them together.
The popping sounds during this process is normal.
Gently remove the bottom panel.
Using too much force can break the plastic clips securing the bottom panel.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HwojqvwAfwRlVX5Y.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CwPnTAgO3disbwIo.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sJlraL3iwM1KueUT.medium)

### 第 3 步

Gently pull the battery connector directly out of its socket.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dGrk5mODqSStqnqD.medium)

### 第 4 步

Using a Phillips #0 screwdriver, remove the 3.0 mm Phillips #0 screws securing the metal Ethernet shielding bracket to the chassis.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/mxlZEKILHpCNU3x5.medium)

### 第 5 步

Lift the metal Ethernet shielding bracket straight up and set it aside.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tXxiMWuElBqtLJD5.medium)

### 第 6 步

Identify the plastic holder securing the RJ-45 Ethernet port in place.
Gently but firmly push the Ethernet port out of the chassis opening.
Do not pull on the cable itself. Grasp the connector housing to avoid damaging the wiring.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/NGncEcfgTfBnYBR1.medium)

### 第 7 步

Locate the ribbon cable or small board connector attaching the Ethernet port assembly to the motherboard.
Disconnect the Ethernet cable from the port assembly by pulling the connector straight out.
Use a plastic spudger if the connector feels tight.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/KxBiRmG1wAIBlQnX.medium)

### 第 8 步

Ta-da!

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1ADB5lUmxpUMloqL.medium)

## 完成
