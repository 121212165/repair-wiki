Use this guide to replace the WWAN card in your Lenovo ThinkPad T480s laptop.

The WWAN (wireless wide area network) card allows you to connect to the internet without relying on a local Wi-Fi connection. If your computer has a WWAN card installed and you are having trouble using the internet without Wi-Fi, you may have a faulty WWAN card or antenna.

If you are having trouble accessing a local Wi-Fi connection, you may need to troubleshoot or replace the Wi-Fi card or WLAN antennas.

Before attempting a replacement, make sure to [link|https://support.lenovo.com/nl/en/solutions/ht510660-removal-and-replacement-videos-thinkpad-t480s-20l7-20l8/|disable the internal battery,] power down your device, and disconnect from any external power sources.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #1 Screwdriver
- iFixit Opening Tool

## 需要的配件

- Lenovo ThinkPad T480s WWAN Card
- 01AX792 - Lenovo Laptop Wireless Module - Genuine
- 01AX761 - Lenovo Laptop WWAN Card - Genuine

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

[link|https://support.lenovo.com/eg/en/solutions/ht510660-removal-and-replacement-videos-thinkpad-t480s-20l7-20l8|Disable the battery], power down the device, and unplug the power adapter from your device before proceeding/beginning the guide.
Loosen the six captive screws with a Phillips #1 screwdriver.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RNt3cWxTIjeD1Mhe.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/haCRyLGBRClsXGrF.medium)

### 第 2 步

Remove the bottom cover with an opening tool.
Start from the top edge near the hinge and work your way along the edge until all of the clips are unsnapped.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Vboy5of3qMKgumu1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Xg36ebBqZsGYEUXf.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lIwLqVaU1qnjeJil.medium)

### 第 3 步

Lift the bottom cover off.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/l4tenwgnMuEBiPRu.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OndiEfoacVynMaGD.medium)

### 第 4 步

Flip the cardboard cover off of the WWAN card.
Using the spudger, release the coaxial antenna wires from the WWAN card.
When reassembling, reattach the blue  wire on the left and the orange wire on the right.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/uvwAoVHDLNELtZMS.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jZvZ1xZXSrVLRGfb.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FuJCGPfxTKnwiBVZ.medium)

### 第 5 步

Use a Phillips #1 screwdriver to remove one 2.8mm screw.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XBZeCiDmFP2vei42.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/aPU5O3ZuFGYKHwIE.medium)

### 第 6 步

Slide the WWAN card out from the slot.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dHFu2XjTJvOZLZH6.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/aARaAbGOhCaQv4sP.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/mjykLjqWLyv3FIZU.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order.
