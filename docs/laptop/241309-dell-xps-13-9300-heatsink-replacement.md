In this guide, I will detail the steps required to remove / replace the heatsink on a Dell XPS 9300.

This guide was completed on a unit equipped with an i7-1065g7 CPU. There may be differences with other CPUs.

## 需要的工具

- T5 Torx Screwdriver
- Phillips #00
- Phillips #1

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Remove bottom cover

Remove the eight Torx T5 screws from the bottom cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vsyjFfMJlsKGtYIs.medium)

### 第 2 步：Remove bottom cover (continued)

Slide a pick (or thin bank card) between the bottom cover and keyboard deck at the corner of the device, near the hinge.
Slide the pick around the entire cover, applying gentle upward pressure to release the plastic clips. Take care not to miss the clips at the rear of the device above the vents.
Lift the cover up and off the laptop.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/XSpeISbvJjwrXdIR.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/WeRgF2RMbuitNRIU.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CSNpWUYEwNN2kASW.medium)

### 第 3 步：Unplug battery connector

On the right side of the main board, below the fan, is the battery connector.
Using a spudger (not a conductive screwdriver) remove the connector from it's housing by pushing on the black plastic tab in it's center.
Move the connector away from the main board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SsMUouGwWkwaIbTg.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/36fH2mQd2CDTBtCX.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lZBdrOBlGBGQHPVm.medium)

### 第 4 步：Unplug fan connectors

Below each fan is a small white connector secured to the main board with tape.
Using a spudger, remove both connectors from their sockets by prying on the small ears on either side.
Unpeel the tape to lift the connectors off the main board. Be careful, as they are stuck on top of small capacitors that can be damaged, and the fan cables are fragile.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sEnEjkIlHcbg2MhN.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sMyPLyvs2fqkcyqD.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SDt2aLwQTOjBO6Jl.medium)

### 第 5 步：Remove fan screws

Using a Phillips #00 bit, remove the two screws holding each fan to the main board.
Be careful not to put too much downward force on the main board, as it can flex easily.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/E6DBnZxqqtVxCliI.medium)

### 第 6 步：Remove heatsink screws

Using a Phillips #1 bit, unscrew the four captive screws holding the heatsink to the CPU.
Remove them in reverse sequential order as stamped on the heatsink legs, starting from four and ending with one. In the photo, unscrew red, yellow, orange then green.
The screws will stay attached to the heatsink.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/nINbZsRmhMycMYmo.medium)

### 第 7 步：Remove heatsink

Lift the heatsink and fans upwards and off the CPU.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qFUeLCCNec4W1Bde.medium)

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow these instructions in reverse order. When reinstalling the heatsink, tighten the screws in the sequence printed on the heatsink legs. Push the bottom cover firlmly once in place to engage the clips.

Always apply fresh thermal paste when reinstalling a heatsink.
