> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #198222](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Lenovo+ThinkPad+T14+Gen+3+Wi-Fi+Card+Replacement/198222) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：15 minutes · 品类：Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 3

In this guide you will learn how to replace the Wi-Fi card in your Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 3. The Wi-Fi card enables your laptop to connect to wireless networks through a router. When within range, your laptop can access the internet unless the network is password-protected. If the Wi-Fi card malfunctions, you may notice issues like failing to detect networks, unstable connections, or complete loss of connectivity. Before beginning, disable the internal battery, power off the laptop, and unplug the charger.

## 需要的工具

- Phillips #1 Screwdriver
- Opening Pick
- Spudger
- Anti-static wrist strap

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Use a Phillips #1 screwdriver to loosen the seven captive screws securing the back panel.
The screws are captive and will not come out of the panel.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iIkcRfyQjOtElYcB.medium)

### 第 2 步

Insert an opening pick into the seam between the back panel and chassis.
Slide the opening pick around the perimeter of the device to release the clips that hold the back panel to the device.
Remove the back panel.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bBdBKpxqc5m2gmMM.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qp4fvjplehmtPSJo.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IEJ1MBMquFUVJGOD.medium)

### 第 3 步

Use a JIS #1 screwdriver to remove six M2 x 5 mm screws that hold the battery in place.
There is a risk to your laptop if the battery is not discharged properly.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gSqgVLUQJMYD5Km4.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1e6FMkIoIkBTPiw3.medium)

### 第 4 步

Slide the battery from the battery connector and remove it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ykCOUKWDVdWHE1vZ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FNOWcdXwbyVsOYnW.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bmpVU3WIYdOOpMCH.medium)

### 第 5 步

With a Phillips #1 screwdriver, remove the 3.6 mm screw located at the top of the Wi-Fi card that secures it to the motherboard.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GPQCmmdW1JTPRCaE.medium)

### 第 6 步

Use a spudger to disconnect the Wi-Fi card coaxial connectors.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/rGaUUBYrQ1Mrqgq4.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/bZpIbAUhrMPxG5cl.medium)

### 第 7 步

Slide the Wi-Fi card out from the slot to remove it from the board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/psbaIlCSZhTdrmGy.medium)

### 第 8 步

Gently pull the Wi-Fi card straight out of its socket.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gFhEdx2dneDwcDBf.medium)

## 完成
