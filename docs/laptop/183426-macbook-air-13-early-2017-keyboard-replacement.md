> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #183426](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/MacBook+Air+13-Inch+Early+2017+Keyboard+Replacement/183426) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：No estimate · 品类：MacBook Air 13" Early 2017

This guide shows how to remove and replace the keyboard of a MacBook Air 13" Early 2017. The procedure requires disconnecting several ribbon cables and wires, removing the logic board, and then unscrewing the 40 screws that secure the keyboard.

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Before proceeding, power down your MacBook. Close the display and lay it on a soft surface, top-side down.
Use a [product|IF145-090|P5 Pentalobe] driver to remove ten screws securing the lower case, of the following lengths:
Two 9 mm screws
Eight 2.6 mm screws

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ntWnApPFnVyDIwGF.medium)

### 第 2 步

Wedge your fingers between the display and the lower case and pull upward to pop the lower case off the Air.
Remove the lower case and set it aside.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/AuaSrWoUvoATMoyN.medium)

### 第 3 步

To ensure that everything is de-energized and won't turn on while you're working, it is recommended that you disconnect the battery.
Grab the clear plastic pull tab attached to the battery connector and pull it parallel to the board toward the front edge of the Air.
Do not lift upward on the connector as you disconnect it or you risk damage to the connector socket.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/LrxfUsj62I2DqUaT.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lAHlQVfBY6CDDY5m.medium)

### 第 4 步

Use the flat end of a spudger to pry the I/O board cable connector up out of its socket on the I/O board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VupXVWMrOWTWVSgZ.medium)

### 第 5 步

Carefully peel the I/O board cable from the adhesive securing it to the top of the fan.
During reassembly, make sure this cable is in the correct orientation. It will fit if reversed, but the laptop will not boot.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TdVfqQIVYKM5Kbms.medium)

### 第 6 步

The following connector has an especially deep socket. Use care when disconnecting it.
While gently pulling the I/O board cable upward near its connection to the logic board, use the flat end of a spudger to pry up on alternating sides of the connector to help "walk" it out of its socket.
Remove the I/O board cable.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jVUTODnP4bqlGEyG.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BbGnHcFhLUSAwXen.medium)

### 第 7 步

Use the tip of a spudger to carefully flip up the retaining flap on the fan cable ZIF socket.
Be sure you are prying up on the hinged retaining flap, '''not''' the socket itself.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YsZsJqpg5u1Djxsr.medium)

### 第 8 步

Peel the rubber gasket off the adhesive on the top of the fan.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UXpYgZwk1S3uNVAF.medium)

### 第 9 步

Remove the following three screws securing the fan to the upper case:
One 5.2 mm T5 Torx screw
One 3.3 mm T5 Torx screw
One 4.4 mm T5 Torx screw with a short head

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iaCMjInercXiolMq.medium)

### 第 10 步

Lift the fan from the I/O board side and pull it free from the upper case.
Removing the fan will also disconnect the fan ribbon cable. Be careful not to snag it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/JOlLlJR1WLw2RFKr.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/puSycLpBrUTdSEnu.medium)

### 第 11 步

Disconnect the I/O board by pulling its power cable away from its socket on the logic board.
Pull the cable parallel to the face of the logic board toward the right edge of the Air.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/n2BSilZF3Tn3WHDd.medium)

### 第 12 步

Use the flat end of a spudger to pry the left speaker cable connector up and out of its socket on the I/O board.
Pry up from beneath the wires.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/T16lCVLQDTTZyC2g.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/4A3thwKjYFEJeh4u.medium)

### 第 13 步

Use the tip of a spudger to carefully flip up the retaining flap on the microphone ribbon cable ZIF socket.
Make sure you are flipping up the retaining flap, '''not''' the socket itself.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cuIRifo1bVAQqBG6.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xMaWVTtyWfQ4xUlK.medium)

### 第 14 步

Remove the single 4.1 mm T5 Torx screw securing the I/O board to the upper case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RiHUpxnQnxdQrnF5.medium)

### 第 15 步

Gently de-route the camera cable from its notch on the I/O board and push it out of the way with the tip of a spudger.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RBLcMgEEY5DEZVgc.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/whLILUyNKrydAqIP.medium)

### 第 16 步

Lift the I/O board from the logic board side and pull it free from the upper case.
Removing the I/O board will also disconnect the microphone ribbon cable. Be careful not to snag it.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/REXwruFdKcMwEUVx.medium)

### 第 17 步

Use the flat end of a spudger to pry the right speaker connector up and out of its socket on the logic board.
Pry up from beneath the wires.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iCQl5ClovjVjJ5Mp.medium)

### 第 18 步

Use the flat end of a spudger to pry the right speaker off the adhesive securing it to the upper case.
Remove the right speaker from the upper case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FpLtPyXYhltALK1q.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FhWhXPeOQT4VpNEM.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/1DrAsrZnMs2WHuC2.medium)

### 第 19 步

Use the flat end of a spudger to pry the left speaker cable connector up and out of its socket on the I/O board.
Pry up from beneath the wires.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/hEADS53autP4Fg1S.medium)

### 第 20 步

Use the flat end of a spudger to pry the left speaker off the adhesive securing it to the upper case.
Remove the left speaker from the upper case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Rbw3wdyWWW2PvoGn.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/W1oAPeguILrl465A.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/iOel1PHocw6iSMeD.medium)

### 第 21 步：Disconnect the display cable

Lift the retaining flap on the display cable connector. You can lift it using a spudger, or by pulling on the black tape attached to it (using your fingers or tweezers).

### 第 22 步：Disconnect the Wi-Fi antenna wires

Insert a comment.

### 第 23 步：Disconnect the camera cable

Pull the cable horizontally to disconnect it. There is no latch or flap on this connector.

### 第 24 步：Disconnect the keyboard and trackpad cables

Flip up the retaining flap on the back of the trackpad connector with the tip of a spudger.
Flip up the retaining flap on the back of the power button connector with the tip of a spudger.
While carefully lifting the keyboard cable with one hand, use the tip of a spudger or your fingernail to flip up the retaining flap on the keyboard cable's ZIF socket.
Be sure to pry up on the hinged retaining flap and not the socket itself.
Pull the keyboard cable straight out of its socket, towards the front edge of the Air.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sOXjtiQsXUAkS2CN.medium)

### 第 25 步：Peel up the backlight assembly

Start at a corner, then work along an edge and pull gently to avoid damaging the backlight if you intend to reuse it.

### 第 26 步：Removing the keyboard

Unscrew the 40 (!) Phillips screws using a Phillips 000 screwdriver that hold the keyboard in place.

### 第 27 步：Disconnect the heat sink from the housing

Remove the two 4.9 mm Torx screws securing the antenna wire retainer for the left display clutch hinge to the upper housing.
Move the antenna wire retainer out of the way and remove the single 3 mm Torx screw securing the end of the heat sink to the upper housing.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/Pp4JUZbALOssHDE5.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3jY6qUjehUfakBLW.medium)

### 第 28 步：Remove the motherboard

Unscrew the 6 Torx screws securing the motherboard.
It is now easy to remove the motherboard. Be careful to clear the Wi-Fi antenna cables from their notches.

## 完成

To reassemble your device, follow the instructions in reverse order.
