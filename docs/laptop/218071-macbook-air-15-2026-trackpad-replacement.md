> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #218071](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/MacBook+Air+15-Inch+2026+Trackpad+Replacement/218071) · 难度：**中等** · 耗时：45 minutes - 1 hour · 品类：MacBook Air 15" 2026

Use this guide to replace the trackpad in your MacBook Air 15" 2026.

You may need to replace the trackpad if it's behaving erratically, unresponsive to touch inputs, or the cursor movement is inconsistent.

## 需要的工具

- iFixit Precision Bit Driver
- P5 Pentalobe Bit
- 5IP Torx Plus Bit
- 3IP Torx Plus Bit
- Spudger
- iOpener
- iFixit Opening Picks (Set of 6)
- ESD Safe Blunt Nose Tweezers
- Suction Handle
- Tesa 61395 Tape
- Hair Dryer

## 步骤

### 第 1 步：Prepare your Macbook Air for disassembly

Allow your MacBook's battery to drain below 25% before starting this repair—a charged battery may catch fire if damaged.
Unplug all cables and fully power off your MacBook.
Close the display and lay your MacBook upside down. Keep your laptop closed until you've physically disconnected the battery.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RdBOkhEeNyU2yrQh.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/f3YY51OXlJcifhAq.medium)

### 第 2 步：Unfasten the lower case

Throughout this repair, [product|IF145-491|keep track of each screw|new_window=true] and make sure it goes back exactly where it came from.
Use a P5 pentalobe screwdriver to remove the four 6.4 mm‑long screws securing the lower case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/VLe1AqG1xhVfa22F.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/MSqb2Idxp2QYIk6B.medium)

### 第 3 步：Insert an opening pick

Apply a suction handle to the center of the lower case's front edge.
Pull up on the suction handle to create a gap between the lower case and the frame.
Insert an opening pick into the gap.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OLHZMoIOgBUBInCQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/oW1DXmHu2xTSOAUg.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/npMpHdfBbNnIjgXL.medium)

### 第 4 步：Release the snapping clips

During the next two steps you'll feel and hear [link|https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/xDYPyPXVaTIFOWry.full|new_window=true|six snapping clips] release. If a clip doesn't release, insert your opening pick farther and try again.
Slide the pick to the bottom right corner to release the first clip.
Slide the pick around the corner and up the right edge to release the next two clips.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/icfXdnPCDqSNIpIL.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sXYsTZUJmWdPIOBO.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/FsveCnI6mB4o6vdZ.medium)

### 第 5 步

Insert the opening pick in the original gap created with the suction handle.
Slide the opening pick to the bottom left corner and up the left edge to release the three remaining snapping clips.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/aOleJq3qgpdhKrNc.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ljfTrGXwPbBtFYvF.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3JqJtEyXMAB4RNYM.medium)

### 第 6 步：Release the sliding tabs

[link|https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/5THwO5bPZQbPB1Qg.full|Four sliding tabs|new_window=true] along the back edge of the MacBook firmly secure the lower case. Separating these tabs requires a lot of force—consider using gloves to protect your hands from the sharp edges of the lower case.
Keep the lower case flat to the frame and firmly slide it straight away from the back edge, one corner at a time, to disengage the sliding tabs.
Don't pull up until the cover is completely separated.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/yUmINYLGDWUvLWTY.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/HoTdfbFiP5TOXsTJ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/DN2rEokJGdNDZsQk.medium)

### 第 7 步：Remove the lower case

Remove the lower case.
During reassembly:
Set the lower case in place and align the sliding tabs with the screw heads they slide over. Press down and slide the lower case toward the back edge to engage the tabs—it'll stop sliding as the tabs engage.
When one side is engaged, it may push the other out of alignment. Check both sides as you push, there should be no gap along the back edge.
Once the lower case is flush with the frame, press down firmly along the perimeter to engage the six snapping clips.
You'll hear and feel a distinct click as each clip snaps into place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UNtWUTpBg4ocEXT4.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/44XcoVjlMduNTrDf.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BATrXQ5vhaJNbZqC.medium)

### 第 8 步：Remove the battery connector cover tape

Use blunt nose [product|IF145-020|tweezers] or your fingers to remove the piece of tape covering the battery connector cover.
During reassembly, use the leftover adhesive to secure the tape to the cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/P2xxSwq3pQFSde6I.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/2aJ3AgWMLtqjwJrs.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/dRRIdlQLRyRbLycf.medium)

### 第 9 步：Remove the battery connector cover

Use a 3IP Torx Plus screwdriver to remove the two 1.5 mm‑long screws securing the battery connector cover.
Remove the cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ytD4SCgCAxXVt24f.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/cejWTHjtXGxlUdlk.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZBvCsV2PVr1eRnd1.medium)

### 第 10 步：Disconnect the battery

Use an opening pick to pry up and disconnect the battery connector.
To reconnect a [link|https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Recognizing+&+Disconnecting+Cable+Connectors/25629#s70308|new_window=true|press connector], align it over the socket and gently press down on one side until it clicks into place, then press down on the other side. It might take a few tries to align the connector.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/mBoddjL4FMGvHdAK.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SxTDekgXBIIXKMgD.medium)

### 第 11 步：Remove the trackpad cable tape

A small piece of black tape hides two trackpad cable cover screws.
Use tweezers or your fingers to remove the tape from the trackpad cable.
During reassembly, use the leftover adhesive to secure the tape to the cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/sauZMAYWojBrwBjW.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tMpetYevVNjXJgYV.medium)

### 第 12 步：Separate the trackpad cable adhesive

Light adhesive secures the trackpad cable to its metal cover.
Pull the trackpad cable away from its cover to separate the adhesive securing it.
During reassembly:
Use the leftover adhesive to secure the trackpad cable to the cover. If the cable doesn't stick, use [product|IF317-072-6|double-sided tape|new_window=true] to secure it.
If you have a replacement trackpad cable with adhesive pre-installed, remove the liner and press the cable into place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OHehkGT4BcGACkcQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/TMMcQtaRDpB2wGXv.medium)

### 第 13 步：Remove the trackpad cable cover

Use a 3IP Torx Plus screwdriver to remove the two 1.5 mm‑long screws securing the trackpad cable cover.
Remove the cover.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/vYusBNNFXAcsMFmT.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gCqE5BhBYQFRcuNb.medium)

### 第 14 步：Disconnect the trackpad cable

Use the point of a spudger to pry up and disconnect the trackpad cable press connector from the logic board.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/QB5xLmoPmoirWDOQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/OWWJTLBHXEqsGFPS.medium)

### 第 15 步：Heat the trackpad cable

[link|https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tGX6gPbEhjlNleVv.full|Two small strips of adhesive|new_window=true] secure the trackpad cable to the frame.
[guide|25705|Heat an iOpener|new_window=true] and apply it to the trackpad cable for two minutes to soften the adhesive securing it to the frame.
You can also use a [guide|157348|hair dryer|new_window=true]—but extreme heat can damage the battery, so proceed with care.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/3NPXt5WQOV5KBFGH.medium)

### 第 16 步：Separate the trackpad cable adhesive

Pull the trackpad cable toward the bottom of the MacBook to separate it from the frame.
During reassembly:
Use the leftover adhesive to secure the trackpad cable to the cover. If the cable doesn't stick, use [product|IF317-072-6|double-sided tape|new_window=true] to secure it.
If you have a replacement trackpad cable with adhesive pre-installed, remove the liners and press the cable into place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/nxE51Fl2P1raMw6G.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BtoWbXgjVpaupUgC.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CYtTESvGjvwIyXLP.medium)

### 第 17 步：Unfasten the trackpad

Use a 5IP Torx Plus screwdriver to remove the ten 4 mm‑long screws securing the trackpad.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/CDAZGq6KTK6QvMMm.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/LAKjvdG5Yst12Uqp.medium)

### 第 18 步：Remove the trackpad

Be careful not to lose the [link|https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kdaJD1nL2PJMtxFG.full|four washers|new_window=true] resting on the trackpad's screw posts. Without them, your trackpad won't fit properly.
Slowly open your MacBook—the trackpad will stay on the display.
Guide the trackpad cable through its slot in the frame.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/lEbyBIooHFyrpum5.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/hUmwjEfxMrXkHiUY.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ODT3vxlueGmYriDi.medium)

### 第 19 步

Remove the trackpad from the display.
Reassembly note:
Before installing your new trackpad, make sure all four washers are in place.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/12iDK1jHvPouTGF1.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/YWNNHTjbsco6HEPQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/kdaJD1nL2PJMtxFG.medium)

### 第 20 步：Transfer the trackpad cable

Before installing your trackpad, make sure it has a cable installed. If your new trackpad doesn't come with a cable, follow the next three steps to remove and transfer the old cable.
Use the tip of a spudger or a clean fingernail to flip up the hinged locking flap on the trackpad cable ZIF connector.
During reassembly:
Secure the cable by flipping the locking flap down.
If your trackpad cable comes with adhesive, remove the plastic liner and press the cable into place on the trackpad.
If the cable doesn't have adhesive, use a strip of [product|IF317-072-6|double-sided tape|new_window=true] to secure it to the trackpad.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/GlFV4COZJawlMngI.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/gU6BLbL2bWVsR6oU.medium)

### 第 21 步

Strong adhesive secures the trackpad cable to the trackpad, just below the cable's connector.
[guide|25705|Heat an iOpener|new_window=true] and apply it to the bottom edge of the trackpad for two minutes to soften the adhesive.
Alternatively, you can  [guide|157348|use a hair dryer|new_window=true] to heat the cable.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/mEYBIVy4yYOxVMYG.medium)

### 第 22 步

Slide an opening pick back and forth under the trackpad cable to separate it from the trackpad.
Pull the trackpad cable out of its socket and remove it.
During reassembly, make sure the locking flap is flipped up before inserting the trackpad cable into its socket.
