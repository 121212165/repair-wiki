> 📖 原教程：[iFixit #214247](https://www.ifixit.com/Guide/Dell+XPS+13+9315+Battery+Replacement/214247) · 难度：**简单** · 耗时：15 minutes · 品类：Dell XPS 13 9315

Use the previous guide to open the back cover of a Dell XPS 13 9315 laptop and use this guide to remove and locate the battery. The battery is responsible for allowing the laptop to be powered without any external power sources while also enabling convenience and portability. In this device, the battery only includes 8 M2x3 screws.

Disconnect all external power sources prior to beginning this guide. Make sure to power off your laptop completely before proceeding with any steps involving the inner components of the laptop.

There are no special skills or tools required to complete this guide.

## 需要的工具

- Opening Pick
- Phillips #0 Screwdriver
- T5 Torx Screwdriver

## 步骤

### 第 1 步

Use a T5 Torx screwdriver to remove the eight 3.2 mm-long screws securing the lower case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jFWCa3AYk2XTUtfn.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/4bKylOrNNZmIqJIx.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/md4XnZJSefOZxuLU.medium)

### 第 2 步

Insert an opening pick into the seam between the lower case and chassis.
Slide the pick around the entire perimeter while prying to disengage the retaining clips.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/IoHugEwDPdydvhVQ.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/qlNZ2xHCP4fSgTLc.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/ZdAPZ2MlI3SB1jHC.medium)

### 第 3 步

The back cover has 13 retention clips attached to the inside of the laptop making it difficult to open the laptop.
Make sure that all clips are disengaged, then remove the lower case.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/PVjFiliHwVX3ig1I.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/SBVJAusFHUyKDof1.medium)

### 第 4 步

Gently walk the battery connector directly out of its socket.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/UREXnyP5MyHfGOdJ.medium)

### 第 5 步

Use a Phillips #0 screwdriver to remove the eight M2x3 screws securing the battery.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/T6pkeSsLRyh66mnK.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/RMf2POygQaW4FWyF.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/jEZdGIekeLnfScDp.medium)

### 第 6 步

Lift the battery up and out of the chassis.

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/BXjjMIKdDDlBsp5Z.medium)

![步骤图](https://guide-images.cdn.ifixit.com/igi/tXWhYNunVTIByAp2.medium)
